Abstract

The UNNS Substrate Theory proposes a Universal Structural Law (USL) — a single inequality, AK(L) ≤ 1, that governs which ordered structures can persist as stable observables. UNNS does not inherit these ideas. It derives its results from empirical structural analysis and finds that the traditions were tracking real geometry.

This article is your bridge. It presents the key concepts, visualizes the core structures, and maps the esoteric resonances — without sacrificing scientific precision or epistemic honesty.

UNNS Is Not Dogma — Falsifiability Statements

What separates UNNS from purely symbolic perennialism is precisely this: it invites disconfirmation. The following specific conditions would falsify the framework. None has been triggered in the 93-dataset corpus.

  • Criterion F1: Find a persistent physical system whose ordered spectrum violates A_K(L) ≤ 1. No such system exists in the corpus. This is not absence of search — it is the result of systematic measurement across 93 datasets.
  • Criterion F2: Show that the realizability class of a system changes under physical conditions without a corresponding change in m(L). This would falsify the margin-phase connection.
  • Criterion F3: Construct two different admissible ladders for the same physical system, both maximising m(L), that yield different realizability classes. This would falsify the maximum-margin principle.
  • Criterion F4: Demonstrate a fifth fundamental interaction requiring a new realizability class beyond {FULL, GIANT, TAIL, HARD}. This would falsify Theorem 5.1 of the interaction unification paper.
  • Criterion F5: Measure a system whose observed scaling (e.g., power law vs exponential) does not match the asymptotic regime predicted by its m(L). This would falsify the scaling bridge.

Previous frameworks have claimed structural universality — the perennial philosophy, the unity of mystical experience, the oneness of being.
