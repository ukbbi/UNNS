03

Audit of the original claims

The phase-inletting and projected-stability statements of the original manuscript (its §5 and §4) were checked against their text. They hold exactly under explicit conditions; the counterexamples locate the boundaries of the mechanisms.

THEOREM A · PHASE INLETTING

Conjugation preserves the class. ρ(RθT) is independent of θ exactly for T ∈ ℂ ∪ ℂ̄ (scaled isometries). supU ρ(UT) = σmax(T).
Counterexample to the unrestricted claim: T = [[1,1],[1,1]] with U = diag(1, −1) gives ρ(T) = 2 but ρ(UT) = 0; U and T do not commute.

THEOREM B · PROJECTED GROWTH

T4.1: with relative error η the orbit stays in an invariant cone; asymptotically limsup |w|/|u| ≤ η/(|λ|−|μ|−η). T4.2: with absolute error h, escape is guaranteed for |u₀| > h/(|λ|−1), and this radius is sharp. Direction is not preserved.

    09

    What is established, what is not, and where ESC-I sits

    DEMONSTRATED

    • An exact, reproducible classification of real 2×2 echoes; the gap between exact class and finite-horizon reading is quantified by two identified traps.
    • Corrected, proved conditions replacing two unrestricted claims of the original manuscript.
    • Admissibility rules can be written, frozen, falsified, repaired and re-confirmed under an explicit protocol.
    • A projection-aware rule predicts the regime of Π∘T for four projection families in the spectral frame, with no observed contradiction.

    NOT DEMONSTRATED

    • That the algebra of complex numbers emerges uniquely from UNNS generative admissibility without its defining structure (J² = −I) being assumed.
    • That the rules exceed classical dynamical-systems theory, or that R beats an equally informed classical analyst.
    • That finite-horizon verdicts are asymptotic theorems.
    • Any correspondence with STRUC-I ladder admissibility or STRUC-PERC-I connectivity; the open percolation-to-admissibility conjecture is not touched.
    • Independent formal proof checking of the theorems.

    THREE INSTRUMENTS, THREE PREDICATES

    Structurally related vocabulary, mathematically distinct objects. No cross-instrument equivalence is established; ρ means spectral radius here and structural pressure in STRUC-I.

    STRUC-I v1.0.4

    Ordered numerical ladders under perturbation.
    inv(Pε; L) ≤ ν(Vε(L))
    Does the perturbed ordering stay within its vulnerability budget?

    STRUC-PERC-I v2.5.0

    Full pairwise vulnerability graphs.
    (i,j) ∈ Eκ ⇔ |Δi − Δj| ≤ ε(κ)
    Does connectivity persist across the scale range?

    ESC-I

    Real recursive states, projected.
    xn+1 = Π(Txn)
    Can the orbit regime be classified or certified?
    A rigorous map from ESC-I trajectories to STRUC-I ladders, and a test of whether projection regimes track ladder admissibility or percolation, would be a new research question; it is not a result of this study.
