\section{Scope tags}

Each result carries a scope tag. \textsf{A0}: elementary occurrence arithmetic, independent of any choice of interaction rule. \textsf{C}: results about the collective map \(C\) of Definition~\ref{th:F.CDEF}, an explicitly selected rule; once \(C\) is selected, its dynamics are theorems.

\section{Correspondence with Volume~I and with the source records}

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item Volume~I, Theorem~1.2 (fibre characterisation) gives \(\Dx\); Theorem~1.7 (square-root reduction) is used in Lemma~\ref{lem:sqrtpart}; Theorem~2.2 (pair future) and Theorem~2.5 (collective rendezvous and coherence) motivate \(G\), \(L\) and the choice of \(C\) in Chapter~\ref{ch:growth}.
\item Source records use \(D(x)\) for \(\Dx(x)\), and \(\mathrm{lcm}(d^2)/G\) for the collective increment; the equality \(\mathrm{lcm}(d^2)=L^2\) is immediate from valuations (Chapter~\ref{ch:growth}). The source records \texttt{F.T48}, \texttt{F.T58}, \texttt{F.T63} appear here as scope remarks (Remarks~\ref{rem:T48},~\ref{rem:T58},~\ref{rem:T63}).
\item The registry chapter field of a record (II.1--II.7) records the order of its original release; the manuscript chapter is given in Appendix~\ref{app:crosswalk}. For example, the \(\gamma\)-framework (\texttt{F.T44}) and the bounded window (\texttt{F.T47}) have registry chapter II.4 and appear in Chapter~\ref{ch:persist}, where they are first used.
\end{itemize}
