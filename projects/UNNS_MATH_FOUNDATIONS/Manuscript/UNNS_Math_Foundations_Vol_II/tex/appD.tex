\chapter{Proof-completeness audit of the volume's records}\label{app:audit}

For each of the 68 records assigned to this volume, the table states where its content stands in the text and how complete the argument given here is. \emph{Full proof}: the statement and a complete proof are in the text, with the hypotheses of the record. \emph{Exact computation}: the claim is a finite exact statement whose certificate is displayed or reproduced by the verifier. \emph{Definition}: the record fixes a rule. \emph{Scope statement} and \emph{Registered correction}: the record states the precise scope of an earlier formulation; it is presented here as a mathematical statement of scope (and, where it corrects a universal claim, the correct theorem is cited). \emph{Open problem}: the record is an open problem and is reported as such. No record is left with only a pointer to an archived proof.
