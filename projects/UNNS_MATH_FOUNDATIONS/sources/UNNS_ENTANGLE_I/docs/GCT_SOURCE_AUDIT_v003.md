# Source Audit for GCT-III v003

Sources reviewed:

- `A First Introduction to the UNNS Substrate` (`unns_first_introduction_final.tex`), section "The Problem Addressed by UNNS": generated occurrence retains rule, position, route, boundary, status; the introductory substrate is `(S,O,D,R,A)`.
- `Mechanisms, Structural Closure, and Proto-Closure in the UNNS Substrate`, Definitions 3–7 and Remark 3: mechanisms, symbolic structural equivalence by renaming/canonicalization, refinement, recurrent closure.
- `Introduction to the UNNS Substrate` (`unns_intro_finished_manuscript.tex`), "Morphisms": exact operator intertwining and bounded approximate-morphism errors.

These are independent UNNS texts rather than the previous GCT papers. They are sufficient to build a countermodel to entailment under *their documented restrictions*. They do not define a quantum observable map, a signed route interaction, or measurement probability. The latter objects have therefore been clearly labeled **proposed extension objects** in this experiment.

The negative verdict is not a claim that every available or possible UNNS formalism has been searched. A separate independently grounded source that explicitly forces interaction factorization through observable projection could change the result.
