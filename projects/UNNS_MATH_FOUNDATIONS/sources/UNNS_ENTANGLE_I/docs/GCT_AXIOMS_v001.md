# GCT Axioms v001

This document freezes the starting assumptions for the **UNNS Entanglement — Generative Correlation Theorem**.

## Core question
Can a UNNS-style generative structure produce Bell-violating correlations without importing the Born rule, a singlet state, or the target correlation formula by hand?

## Frozen assumptions

### A1. Bell scenario
Two observers choose local settings \(a\) and \(b\). Outcomes are binary: \(+1\) or \(-1\).

### A2. Shared generative structure
A single latent structural object \(\lambda\) may influence both wings.

### A3. Measurement independence
\[
\rho(\lambda\mid a,b)=\rho(\lambda).
\]

### A4. Bell-local factorization
\[
P(A,B\mid a,b,\lambda)=P(A\mid a,\lambda)P(B\mid b,\lambda).
\]

### A5. No-signalling requirement
Any candidate observable model must satisfy local-marginal independence from the remote setting.

### A6. Forbidden imports
The generator may not use the singlet-state formula or any equivalent Born-rule distribution. Those are reserved for a quarantined comparator.

## Consequence
Under these assumptions, a Bell-local no-go result is expected. This is intentional: it tells us exactly what must change before UNNS can claim an entanglement derivation.
