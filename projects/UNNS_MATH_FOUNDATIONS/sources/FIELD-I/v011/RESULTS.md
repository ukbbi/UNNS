## Error found and fixed during v011 (registered)
The first full run had one soundness mismatch (g = 127, E = {2:2, 3:2, 11:5, 557:7}). Cause: my `predicted_word` listed the repeating gap word starting at the smallest element of Abs instead of the **current phase**; the theorem was unaffected. Almost all certified seeds have constant gaps, which had hidden the bug (only 2 of 310 had a non-constant pattern).
Fix: the word is generated by walking e ↦ e + gap(e) from the current phase; test S2b was added to target non-constant patterns (11 of 442). The final run is the corrected one.
