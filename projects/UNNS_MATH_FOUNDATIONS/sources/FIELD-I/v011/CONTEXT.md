## What v011 established (THEOREMS.md T64–T68)

2. **T64 minimax.** For fixed odometer tables the survivor is argmin_p m_p, m_p=p^{largest cyclic gap of Abs_p}, independent of phases; admissible forever iff m_min<W.
3. **T65 quotient formula.** Abs_p=S_p+t_pZ from (Z/g)*/H (H generated by saturated primes, F = frozen divisor residues); t_p=o_p/gcd(o_p,|H|) for prime g; also valid for composite g.

5. **T68** every persistent orbit reaches a certified reset state; g alone does not determine the survivor.
6. Data: 310/400 random reset seeds are certified at x0 itself; end-to-end static prediction of survivor, γ-period and ρ equals the exact class-closure cycle (124/124).
