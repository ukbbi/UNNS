# FIELD-I — precise limitations

- Every occurrence in F1–F5 has positive integer modulus and nest. Allowing zero, negative, real or complex inputs is a different problem.
- The rational-fibre theorem is elementary number theory from the original formula. Novelty relative to existing square-divisor, congruence and Diophantine literature is not established.
- The 2017 founding blog provides the fixed-nest arithmetic definition; an ambiguous historical cross-nest passage is not used as an independent theorem.
- The two-point chart `G=(S_N(M),S_N(M+1))` explicitly reveals the nest label through subtraction. This cannot be presented as a novel, non-leaky mechanism of relational measurement.
- The fixed-nest successor is unary. It does not supply a binary joint-continuation law. `J_low` and `J_high` are counterexample constructions, not physically or grammatically privileged operators.
- The term "rational field" here means non-anchor positive rational outputs; it makes no statement about physical fields.
- A finite verification suite does not establish originality, predictive novelty, or physical relevance. No claim is made about quantum entanglement or no-signalling.
- SELECT-II self-iteration `m->m*(N+1)^2/N` with variable N is a separately defined extension, not the founding fixed-N successor.
