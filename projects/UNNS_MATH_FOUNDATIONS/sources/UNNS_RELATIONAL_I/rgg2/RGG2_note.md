Setting: anchor route r=(k,d), d=N+1≥2, v(r)=kd². J_join(r,s)=(lcm k, lcm d). Fibre F(m)={r: v(r)=m}, |F(m)|=R(m).

## 1. Results
**T1 (order structure) [proved].** Under componentwise divisibility on (k,d), J_join is the least upper bound, hence commutative/associative/idempotent; v(r) | v(r∨s) and v(s) | v(r∨s). Fibres are antichains (r | r', v(r)=v(r') ⇒ k=k', d=d').

**T2 (exact join value) [proved].** For r,r'∈F(m), g=gcd(d,d'), L=lcm(d,d'): lcm(k,k')=m/g² (per prime: max(e−2a, e−2a')=e−2min(a,a')), so
  v(r∨r') = m·(L/g)².

**T3 (universal fibre separation) [proved].** r≠r' in F(m) ⇒ d≠d' ⇒ L>g ⇒ v(r∨r')>m, while v(r∨r)=m. So probe s=r always separates r from every other route of its fibre. Every nontrivial nest fibre is resolved by value-level joint continuation. (Rule-imposed consequence of J_join, not evidence of physical interaction.)

**T4 (relational distance) [proved].** D(r,r') := log(v(r∨r')/m) = 2log(L/g) = 2Σ_p |ν_p(d)−ν_p(d')| log p: a weighted L¹ metric on the fibre. Fibre geometry: F(m) ≅ ∏_p {0..⌊e_p/2⌋} minus the origin (d=1), weights 2log p; |F|=∏(⌊e/2⌋+1)−1=R(m). Diameter = 2Σ⌊e_p/2⌋log p if ≥2 relevant primes, else 2(⌊e/2⌋−1)log p [diameter formula checked, m≤6000]. Fibre of 72: (18,2),(8,3),(2,6) → join values 2592, 648, 288 (ratios 36, 9, 4).

**T5 (refinement) [proved + checked].** T_q(k,d)=(k/q²,dq) preserves value. (a) J_join(T_q r, T_q s)=T_q J_join(r,s) (lcm scales exactly) — joint covariance; hence value of the joint continuation is jointly refinement-invariant. (b) D is invariant under joint refinement; one step costs D(T_q r, r)=2 log q. (c) One-argument invariance fails: v(join(T_q r,s))≠v(join(r,s)) in 5098/10304 tested cases. (d) **Refinement-geodesic = D [proved, checked on 3581 pairs]:** go via the fibre route with d=lcm(d,d') (exists since ⌊e/2⌋ bounds each coordinate; avoids d=1), cost Σ(max−a)+Σ(max−a')·2log p = D; the L¹ lower bound gives equality. So the relational distance equals the minimal refinement cost between the two routes — three descriptions (join excess, weighted L¹, refinement path) coincide.

**T6 (set-theoretic quotient criterion, no bilinearity) [proved].** For single-valued J and observation Π, say Π is J-congruent if Π(r)=Π(r') ⇒ Π J(r,s)=Π J(r',s) and symmetrically. Let ~_R be the equivalence generated by pairs (r,T r), and assume (H2′) ~_R = ~_Π. Then J descends to Π iff Π J(Tr,s)=Π J(r,s) for every generating T,r,s (and in slot 2). Proof: for fixed s, {(r,r'): ΠJ(r,s)=ΠJ(r',s)} is an equivalence relation; it contains ~_R iff it contains the generators. [proved; nonlinear analogue of Thm 7.1]. (H2′) holds for anchors: fibre graph connected by prime refinements [checked m≤6000 with the corrected prime list; proof in T5(d)]. Consequence: J_mul descends (check 6c), J_join does not (T5c).
  Set-valued version: replace ΠJ by the image set Π_*J. For bilinear B with scalar output this reduces to K∩Rad_L (remark, one-step contexts only).
  **Set deficit:** δ_set(m)=#(≈-classes in F(m))−1 where ≈ is the largest J-congruence inside ~_Π. J_join: δ_set=R(m)−1 (all routes pairwise separated, T3). J_mul with value observation: δ_set=0 [checked].

**T7 (excess is not a function of values) [checked + proved by example].** ε(r,s)=v(r∨s)/lcm(v(r),v(s)) is an integer; ε=1 when r|s. 35687 value pairs (a,b) admit more than one ε, e.g. (4,36): ε∈{1,4}. So the join carries genuinely route-level relational information invisible to the values — the nonlinear counterpart of δ>0, with no B assumed.

## 2. Adversarial alternatives
- **Meet (gcd,gcd)** is partial (needs gcd(d,d')≥2): undefined for 501/2745 same-value pairs (an exclusion zone). When defined, v(meet)·v(join)=m² [proved/checked].
- **Mixed ops** (lcm k, gcd d) and (gcd k, lcm d) are value-preserving on a fibre: they return the fibre routes (m/g²,g) and (m/L²,L) — the grid meet and join *inside* the fibre; so the fibre is a lattice, and J_join leaves it exactly by D. On foreign probes both leak heavily (59053/71512; 22633/28424).
- **Coordinate sensitivity.** J_join in (k,N) and in occurrence coordinates (M,N) (anchor-closed [proved: lcm N | lcm M]) also separate every tested fibre pair (1277/1277, v>m). So *separation is robust to the coordinate choice*; the exact formula T2 and metric T4 are specific to (k,d) coordinates (value = kd²). Leakage there: 63112/71512 and 64512/71512.
- **Probe complexity [checked, not proved].** In all 693 fibres with ≤30 routes (m≤6000) a single probe from the fibre resolves it.

## 3. Critical assessment
- The new results are T2–T6; T3 is the clean headline. Honest caveat: they are consequences of choosing the lub in the divisibility order; they hold *because* J_join is a semilattice operation.
- Justification of J_join as a UNNS mechanism **[open]**: only "least common route extending both in the nest's own divisibility structure". No independent UNNS source or physical constraint selects it over (k,N) or (M,N) variants, which behave qualitatively the same; this is a robustness point in favour of the *phenomenon*, against the claim that J_join specifically is canonical.
- The refinement-geodesic identity (T5d) is the most structural finding: relational distance = refinement cost.
- Not established: any bilinear/B representation of J_join (tropical), any field-occurrence rule, anything physical.
