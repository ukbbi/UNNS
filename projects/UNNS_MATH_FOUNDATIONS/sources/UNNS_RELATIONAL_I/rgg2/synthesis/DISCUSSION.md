# RGG-2 Discussion and limitations

The useful gain is a mathematically sharp *conditional* result: given a route-sensitive continuation `J_join`, no nontrivial integer-anchor value fibre remains behaviorally indistinguishable under all anchor probes. This is stronger than a sampled leakage percentage. The candidate operation also yields an exact within-fibre geometry without postulating a bilinear interaction.

**Selection problem:** lcm is a natural join in the divisibility order, but the nest formula alone does not show why a physical or generative system would implement that join instead of multiplication, gcd (partial), or another rule. Passing definitional checks is not independent validation. The four proposed operations have different closure and congruence properties, which are to be compared adversarially.

**Refinement caveat:** positive-anchor refinements are partially defined affine translations in exponent coordinates; equivalence with the manuscript's globally linear refinement theorem is not automatic. Further work must construct a defensible extension before citing the theorem literally.

**Out of scope:** non-anchor occurrences, evolution `F`, bilinear pairing `B`, quantum probabilities, no-signalling and entanglement. Revisiting these requires justified additional modeling decisions.
