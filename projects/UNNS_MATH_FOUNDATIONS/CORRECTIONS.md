# Corrections and limitations for manuscript citation

Preserve the exact historical releases. Use later qualifications, errata and counterexamples when referring to older results.

- **Interaction nonselection:** SELECT-II self-substitution and FIELD-I collective map `C` are additional mathematical choices; original source arithmetic does not uniquely mandate them. See [FIELD-I v002 theorems](sources/FIELD-I/v002/THEOREMS.md).
- **Universal collapse to `G=1`:** superseded by persistent regimes from [v007](sources/FIELD-I/v007/THEOREMS.md) and later results.
- **Universal three-step/fixed squarefree phases:** falsified by [v009 T50/T58](sources/FIELD-I/v009/THEOREMS.md); universal scaling remains in T51 with variable period and multiplier.
- **Multi-prime recurrent carries:** [v010 T60–T61](sources/FIELD-I/v010/THEOREMS.md) establishes single-prime recurrence within the selected collective dynamics.
- **Static certification:** [v011](sources/FIELD-I/v011/THEOREMS.md) gives conditional certificates; its T68(a) general phrasing has a qualification recorded in [v012 T73](sources/FIELD-I/v012/THEOREMS.md), especially for composite/even `g`.
- **Transient bounds:** [v012 T69–T72](sources/FIELD-I/v012/THEOREMS.md) distinguish carry events from ordinary collective iterations and provide explicit upper bounds. Bounds are worst-case and are not statements of exact transient lengths.
- **Ongoing/unfinished:** do not turn finite data or unfinished certificate runs into impossibility theorems or universal constructions. Refer to the original RESULTS and CONTEXT files.

This list is a citation aid, not a replacement for the theorem statements and their original hypotheses.
