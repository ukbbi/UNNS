# SCR-I v003 Hypotheses and Outcome

## H1 — Candidate contraction

Frozen G1→G4 yields positive candidate reduction. **Supported:** median reduction ≈92.03%.

## H2 — Basin/class preservation above adequate sampling density

The independently defined viable mechanism basin/class remains represented under all frozen geometric criteria. **Supported at N=10,000 and N=40,000; not supported at N=2,500 due representation loss.** Basin recall itself remains 1.0 in all cells.

## H3 — Net computational gain

Filter + downstream cost is lower than baseline. **Supported in the frozen calibration:** median time gain ≈8.39× and median operation gain ≈6.73×.

## H4 — Structural selectivity

SCR survivors are enriched for independently viable targets relative to rate-matched random pruning. **Supported:** median enrichment ≈1.89×; observed SCR target recall exceeds every sampled matched-null maximum in all 15 cells.

## H5 — Unique geometric preservation

SCR alone preserves the basin geometry. **Not supported.** At sufficiently large N, rate-matched random pruning also preserves the broad basin geometry well.

## H-UPI — Independent instability association

Still untested in the primary run. UPI remains a blinded secondary diagnostic and must not be retroactively used to define the v003 pruning set.
