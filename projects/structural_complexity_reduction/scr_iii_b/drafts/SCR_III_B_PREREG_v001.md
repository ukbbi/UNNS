## 1. Ordering of hypotheses (not interchangeable)

### H-G (primary scientific object): Generative grammar contraction

A frozen target-blind vertex grammar can **construct** its admitted configurations while avoiding most nominal state materializations. Primary endpoints: exact survivor cardinality, constructive node visits, cost of generation, and matching independent brute-force membership.

**Falsify or limit H-G** if independently counted constructive membership differs, grammar uses target energy/labels, or the alleged contraction is only a post-hoc scan rather than direct generation. **Provisional evidence exists from v007** (2,604 of 16,777,216), so the 3×3 count is a **replication/structural reference**, not a new discovery.

### H-P (co-primary physical condition): Independent target preservation

For a prospectively declared physical target window `S_T(W)`, every target is produced by the grammar: `S_T(W)⊆S_G`; false exclusions must equal **zero**.

**Falsify H-P for a window** upon one excluded legitimate target, incomplete exact verification, or a target rule adjusted after observing the answer. A G PASS may coexist with a P FAIL.

### H-T (separate future physical transfer): DEFERRED, NOT TESTED

The former H-T required exact preservation of a fixed full energy-window target *not explained by the first non-ice gap*. **That requirement is impossible**. With `Δ_nonG=min_{s∉S_G}(H(s)-E0)` for the inclusive target `S_T(W)={s:H(s)<=E0+W}`, exact preservation is equivalent to `W<Δ_nonG` (unless the grammar admits every state, in which case the exclusion minimum is `+∞`). The equality boundary fails preservation. All fully preserved energy windows are gap-aligned by definition; a different Hamiltonian with the same target construction does not resolve this logic.

**Replacement scope:** H-T means generalization to a *different independently specified physical target family*, not an extra criterion on current 3×3 W-windows. Candidate future target: dynamical persistence under a separately frozen physical evolution rule and observation interval, constructed without conditioning on ice membership. Alternative target types require an equally explicit protocol. Freeze its sampling universe, independent labels, holdout sets, nulls, physical controls, and whether the test is exact-set preservation or prospective partial-recall inference. **No H-T test is authorized by the present preregistration.** Report `T=NOT_TESTED / PROTOCOL_UNFROZEN`; do not award `TRANSFER_CONFIRMED` from energy-window alignment.

### H-C (tertiary): Charged computational consequence

The grammar-constrained method completes the identical physical task with lower total charged cost than the optimized exhaustive Gray-code baseline B0 (B1 branch-and-bound reported as sensitivity, not a second mandatory denominator) under frozen timing/operation metrics and uncertainty rules.

H-C **does not define** H-G, H-P, or H-T. Lack of speedup is a C FAIL, not a retroactive G FAIL. For the *complete target-set task*, C is conditional on an independently established H-P PASS; otherwise G2 returns only `S_T∩S_G` and is not an equal-output comparator. The exhaustive preservation audit is separately reported as a research-certification cost, with any repeated deployment certification charged to deployment costs.

## 2. Exact experimental task and target

Primary patch: 3×3 free-boundary square ice, 24 spins, 16,777,216 nominal configurations, with **unchanged v005 Hamiltonian**. The v007 ground anchor (W=0) is known and excluded from novel transfer claims.

Fixed task for B0/B1/G1/G2: **return the full target-configuration ID set** satisfying `H(s)≤E0+W`. Tie and tolerance rules must be declared before any W>0 disclosure. Direct physical energies certify the target; Gray-code incremental energy must be cross-checked against direct calculations.

**UNRESOLVED — MUST FREEZE:** independent formula for `W` and every numerical constant. Admissible options in the source memo include `W=c A(T_ref)`, `W=c k_B T_ref`, or a carefully specified level-respecting cardinality rule. Do not select `c` using ice membership, `Δ_nonG`, or observed 3×3 outcomes. Record reference temperature and energy units.

**Gap diagnostic (reported after freeze and unblinding).** For the real grammar and each valid null: `Δ_G=min_{s∉S_G}[H(s)−E0]`; where `Δ_G>0`, `ρ_G=W/Δ_G`; `ρ_G<1` passes, `ρ_G≥1` fails; `Δ_G=0` and the empty excluded set are separate classes.

**Thermal-window disclosure.** A window `W=k_BT` (792 K, 1.0935e-20 J) exceeds the known 1×1 and 2×2 exclusion gaps (0.871 and 0.952 k_BT; ρ_G≈1.15 and 1.05). 3×3 preservation failure is a genuine possibility and is reported as such; the window is not to be changed to avoid it.

**Tie policy (proposed, not frozen).** Membership `E≤E0+W+τ`, `τ=1e-9·Σ|K|`, `PRESERVED ⇔ W+τ<Δ`; tolerance never converts an excluded target into a preserved one. Freeze before inspecting real 3×3 thresholds.

### 12.1 Common ground-energy reference
Every solver (B0, B1, G1, G2, E2) receives the same frozen v007 anchor E0 and thresholds `Ecut = E0 + W + τ`. A grammar-restricted minimum Ê0 is never substituted for E0 (G1/G2/E2 cannot self-certify E0). Certification cost is reported separately (precertified and end-to-end scenarios).

### 12.2 Numerical policy (three distinct quantities)
Evaluation error (`eps_direct`, `eps_gray`), the membership tolerance `τ = 1e-9·Σ|K|` (the only quantity that enlarges the target) and the level-grouping tolerance used for reporting degenerate levels are different things. Near-threshold states receive a higher-precision (binary128) evaluation; `|d| ≤ 2^-52 Q` is UNRESOLVED_BOUNDARY and is conservatively counted as target. PRESERVED ⇔ `W + τ < Δ_nonG`; the equality case fails. The v007 post-hoc drift revision (1e−12 → 1e−10) is not a precedent.

### 12.3 Windows and prior-information disclosure
Primary `W = k_B T_ref` (T_ref = 792 K, k_B = 1.380649e-23 J/K, 1.093474008e-20 J (the earlier wording 1.093473608e-20 J was a transcription typo, relative 3.7e-7)); sensitivity windows 0.5 and 2 k_B T. The public small-patch gaps already exceed the primary window: 1×1 Δ_nonG = 0.871 k_BT (ρ_G ≈ 1.15), 2×2 Δ_nonG = 0.952 k_BT (ρ_G ≈ 1.05). Preservation at 3×3 may fail; if it does, that result is reported and no replacement primary window may be chosen. Δ_nonG for the 3×3 patch is not computed before the freeze.

### 12.4 Nulls
N1 (degree-preserving) and N2 (free incidence); master seed 20261010; 1,000 valid nulls per family; derivation `'<seed>|<family>|<idx>'` (identical to frozen v006); attempted / accepted / duplicate / rejected / exhausted draws are recorded separately; nulls are never ranked or selected by target performance. After unblinding, report Δ_null, W/Δ_null and the exact survivor count per null.

### 12.5 Ordering, symmetry, cost and timing
Deterministic orders are specified per solver (see `README_v009.md`). Z₂ global-reversal symmetry is disabled in the primary experiment; an optional sensitivity analysis must give every solver the same opportunity and charge all costs. Cost ledger: setup + generation + filter + energy + other, each operation once, with E0 certification separate. Timing: 1 thread, 3 warm-ups, 15 timed rounds, balanced interleaving, median and dispersion; limits 30 min per arm and 2 GiB.

### 12.6 Axis decision table
G: exact constructive = independent membership, no leakage, ≥ 99% contraction (non-discriminating given the known 2,604/2^24). P: FN = 0 with a complete exact verifier. T: NOT_TESTED / PROTOCOL_UNFROZEN. C: only if P passed and completeness is certified; ≥ 1.20× median speedup against optimised B0 with the lower end of the paired 95% bootstrap interval ≥ 1.20; B1 and E2 are reported as sensitivity arms.

### 12.7 Blinding
3×3 W>0 unblinding is forbidden until a separate freeze and authorization. The v009 invocation audit records every executable invocation; blocked attempts are recorded and are not results. The study is low-energy-outcome blinded, not spectrum-blind.
