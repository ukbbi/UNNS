# Refinement References for the Affine Step

1. Gary Brookfield, **Cancellation in Primely Generated Refinement Monoids**,
   *Algebra Universalis* 46 (2001), 343–371.
   DOI: 10.1007/PL00000350.

   Relevant structural results:
   - finitely generated refinement monoids are primely generated;
   - stably finite / cancellative primely generated refinement monoids reduce to
     a free commutative part (with a group factor in the general non-conical case).

2. Pere Ara and K. R. Goodearl, **Tame and Wild Refinement Monoids**,
   *Semigroup Forum* 91 (2015), 1–27.
   arXiv:1405.7582.

   Relevant background:
   - refinement implies Riesz decomposition;
   - in cancellative conical settings refinement/decomposition/interpolation align
     with the corresponding ordered-group structure.

The project theorem file gives a self-contained proof specialized to positive affine
monoids; these references establish that the result is aligned with standard refinement
monoid theory rather than being an ad hoc UNNS construction.
