WHAT STRUC-I DOES

STRUC-I is a structural interrogation engine. It evaluates how ordered numerical structures respond to perturbations and tests whether their internal ordering remains admissible.

It operates on ordered ladders — sequences such as:

  • Atomic energy levels
  • Molecular spectra
  • Nuclear transition schemes
  • Gravitational harmonic fields
  • Cosmological spectra
  • Or any structured numeric dataset

The chamber is domain-agnostic. It operates on the geometry of ordering — not on the physical domain.

Prebuilt corpus packs are reference datasets — not limitations.
