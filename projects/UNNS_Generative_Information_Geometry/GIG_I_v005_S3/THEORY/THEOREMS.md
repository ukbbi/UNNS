# GIG-I v005 Stage 3 — Dimensional lifting of arithmetic homology obstructions (H₂ → H₃)

Notation. Ranks 0..5 are the six labels of a content λ, rank 6 is a new largest label x. For an ordered partition f=(B₁|…|B_s) of {0..5}:
Ins_i(f) adds rank 6 to block i; Λ(f)=Σ_i Ins_i(f); first(f)=({6}|f); last(f)=(f|{6}); all extended linearly over F₂. For a 2-cycle z of P₆ and 3-chains c₁,c₂ of P₆ with ∂c₁=∂c₂=z:
 **L_{c₁,c₂}(z) = Λ(z) + first(c₁) + last(c₂)**  (two-sided suspension).

**LT-0 (boundary lemma) [PROVED; also checked for every face of P₆].** ∂Λ(f) = Λ(∂f) + first(f) + last(f) (mod 2); ∂first(g)=first(∂g), ∂last(g)=last(∂g).

**LT-1 (the lift is a cycle) [PROVED].** L_{c₁,c₂}(z) is a 3-cycle of P₇ for every 2-cycle z and every pair of fillings. (Checked: 700 lifts built from the 32 Stage-2 classes, all cycles.)

**LT-2 (diagonal obstruction) [PROVED].** L_{c,c}(z) = ∂Λ(c), and every prefix set of every 4-face of Λ(c) lies in Pref(first(c)) ∪ Pref(last(c)) ⊆ P_{L_{c,c}(z)}. Hence a diagonal lift bounds in every history complex that contains it: **it never carries a hole.** Non-trivial H₃ requires c₁ ≠ c₂ (e.g. fillings through different minimal families).
 Identity [PROVED]: L_{c₁,c₂}(z) = ∂Λ(c₁) + last(c₁+c₂): the lift is homologous (when Λ(c₁) is present) to the 3-cycle w=c₁+c₂ of P₆ placed in the facet (P₆|{6}), i.e. to "two different fillings of z glued along z".

**LT-3 (cochain lift) [PROVED for the pairing; obstruction set FINITE-EXACT].** Let φ₂ be a 2-cochain all of whose support faces contain the same block B* and put φ₃ = Σ_{f∈supp φ₂} Ins_{B*}(f). Then φ₃(L_{c₁,c₂}(z)) = φ₂(z) for all c₁,c₂ (Λ(z) has no collisions; φ₃ vanishes on first(·), last(·)). For the Stage-1 pair (z, φ₂), φ₃ has exactly 6 four-faces with odd boundary pairing in P₇, ({a,2,4,5,6}|y|w) — the six obstructed octahedra of P₆ with rank 6 inserted in the first block. φ₃ with rank 6 inserted in *every* block does not work (pairing 0, 12 obstructed faces).

**LT-4 (reconstruction of the four H₃ examples) [FINITE-EXACT].** With B0={21}, B1={34,38,46} (the two small minimal fillings of Stage 1) and fillings c_a: the four inherited certificates H3-1…H3-4 are generated by the lift: H3-2, H3-4 (x=15) by (B0,B1), H3-1, H3-3 (x=14) by (B1,B0). In each case P_{z₃} coincides with the certificate's (91 prefix sets), the 4 minimal filling families coincide ([1,3,31,33] sizes), and in the regime complex X_λ(m) the certified z₃ and the lift differ by a boundary (β₃=1, φ₃∘∂₄=0 verified).

**LT-5 (all 16 lifts of the Stage-1 cycle) [FINITE-EXACT, complete certificates].** For the 4 diagonal pairs the minimal family is ∅ (degenerate, LT-2). For the 12 ordered off-diagonal pairs the minimal filling families in P₇ (4-cell boundaries, q ≤ 38 free prefix sets of 127) are computed exactly with an independently checked completeness certificate: sizes (0,1)/(1,0): [1,3,31,33]; (0,2)/(2,0): [1,7,15,21]; (0,3)/(3,0): [2,2,4,16,18,18]; (1,2)/(2,1): [2,4,4,16,16,18]; (1,3)/(3,1): [1,3,15,17]; (2,3)/(3,2): [1,3,3,5]. Reversal (a,b)→(b,a) preserves the multiset of sizes but not P_{z₃}.

**LT-6 (non-degeneracy across mechanisms) [FINITE-EXACT, not proved in general].** For each of the 32 Stage-2 law classes, every one of the 554 ordered off-diagonal pairs (a≠b) gives a non-degenerate lift (it does not bound in the complex generated by its own prefix sets); all 146 diagonal lifts are degenerate, as LT-2 requires.

**AT-2′ (existence in any dimension) [PROVED].** For a d-cycle z of P_k with minimal filling families 𝔉(z), a source m with [z]≠0 exists iff no F∈𝔉(z) lies in A(K_{P_z}). (Same monotonicity proof as Stage 2.)

**AS-1 (arithmetic survival of the lifts; BOUNDED).** Identity role map, all 7-label contents with labels in [2,30] (1,560,780 contents), 12 patterns: 202 contents admit a hole regime of at least one lifted pattern (by pattern: 01:70, 02:39, 03:13, 10:77, 12:22, 20:46, 21:25, 30:18; none for 13, 23, 31, 32), against the 4 contents found at labels ≤ 18 by v004. All 214 predicted regimes (all patterns for 25 contents, first pattern otherwise) were verified on the full regime complexes: z₃ present, not a boundary, β₃ ∈ {1 (198), 2 (6), 3 (9), 4 (1)}, with an exact cochain; 100 random predicted negatives were verified to bound. 200 of the 202 contents have a six-label sub-content with a Stage-1 H₂ hole (the 2 exceptions have x=30). Orientation vs parity of x: odd x mostly select (a,b) with a<b, even x the reverse (167 of 202); not a theorem.

**Not claimed.** (i) That non-degeneracy holds for every cycle of P₆ (only for the 32 classes). (ii) Arithmetic realisability of the lifts of the other 31 classes (not censused). (iii) Role maps other than the identity on the 7 ranks. (iv) That every H₃ regime arises from a lift. (v) Independence of the lift from the chosen fillings c_a within a family.
