# GIG-INFO v001 — Information Recovery Across Arithmetic Topological Transitions
Tags: [PROVED] · [FINITE-EXACT] exact computation on the stated finite objects · [OPEN]. GF(2) homology; uniform ensembles are explicit experimental conventions, not probability laws of the grammar.

**Setting.** Content C of 6 distinct labels, source m. Admissible histories = arrangements of C admissible from m (N(m) of them); swap graph = induced permutohedron graph; K_A(m) its permutohedral complex. Anchored state: Y(m)=1 iff the generator [z] of H₂(K_A(m₀)) (β₂=1) maps to a non-zero class in H₂(K_A(m); Y=0 (filled) if it maps to 0; Y='NA' if A(m)⊉A(m₀). Anchors: A1 = (2,3,4,5,8,9), m₀=288; A2 = (2,3,6,7,8,9), m₀=432.

- **GI-0 [PROVED]** A(m)={I: D(I)|m} depends on m only through min(v_p(m), cap_p) for the primes p dividing some D(I) (the primes of the labels and of label+1). A1: caps 2⁶,3³,5¹ (56 system classes); A2: 60 classes. Hence the whole source line collapses to a finite lattice of systems, and “the 60 sources” realise only 8 of them.
- **GI-1 [FINITE-EXACT]** The anchored definition reproduces the stated survival laws: A1 survives exactly for m=288t, gcd(t,30)=1 (16/60); A2 for m=432t, gcd(t,42)=1 (17/60). Every comparable class carries an independently verified certificate (a 6-face cochain for the survivor, an explicit filling chain for each filled class).
- **GI-2 [FINITE-EXACT] Exact survival entropy.** H(Y)=−p log₂p−(1−p)log₂(1−p): A1 p=4/15, 0.83664 bits; A2 p=17/60, 0.85995 bits (uniform t=1..60).
- **GI-3 [FINITE-EXACT] Information-recovery profile on the 60-source ensembles.** For both anchors, H(Y|O_i)=0 for i=0,1,2,2c,3: the history count alone already recovers the hole state, I(Y;O₀)=H(Y). The reason is structural: on the comparable window the 8 classes have 8 distinct history counts (A1: 372,408,444,480,612,648,684,720; A2: 276,312,324,360,612,648,684,720). There is therefore **no certified pair of sources with equal history entropy and different hole state in the 60-source experiment** (A1 or A2), and none among all comparable sources.
- **GI-4 [FINITE-EXACT] Beyond the comparable window.** Over all system classes (every source), A1 has no binary separating pair at any level. A2 has one at O₀: m=648 (N=276, anchor class undefined) vs m=432 (N=276, survives); it is separated at O₁ (radius-1/2 ball histograms). This pair is not a “filled vs survives” pair: 648 is not a multiple of 432, so Y(648) is NA, counted as 0 only by the binary convention.
- **GI-5 [FINITE-EXACT] Uncolored data can fail.** A1: m=2160 (NA) and m=2880 (filled) have isomorphic uncolored swap graphs, equal ball histograms, equal Betti and f-vectors; they are the same abstract complex under the rank relabelling σ=(2,0,1,3,5,4) of A(m); only the labelled arithmetic data (O₃) separates them. A2 has no such pair.
- **GI-6 [FINITE-EXACT] Counting law.** A1: N(288t)=372+36[2|t]+72[3|t]+240[5|t] (additive). A2: N(432t)=276+36[2|t]+48[3|t]+336[7|t] fails additivity: the 3·7 interaction is +24 (684 vs 660, 720 vs 696).
- **GI-7 [FINITE-EXACT] Arrow law (divisibility m→mp, not time).** On the comparable window, multiplying by a prime of the modulus (A1: 2,3,5; A2: 2,3,7) sends Y=1→0 at once and Y=0 stays 0: the filled state is absorbing. Exact information jumps from the surviving source: A1 ΔH_hist = log₂(408/372), log₂(444/372), log₂(612/372) = 0.1333, 0.2553, 0.7182 bits for ×2, ×3, ×5; A2 log₂(312/276), log₂(324/276), log₂(612/276) = 0.1769, 0.2313, 1.1489 bits for ×2, ×3, ×7. Multiplying beyond the cap leaves A, N and Y unchanged.
- **GI-8 [OPEN]** Whether history count injectivity on the comparable window is a theorem (it is a table here); a general-grammar version of the observation hierarchy for non-integer UNNS systems; the role of the uniform-t convention (other ensembles change H(Y) and all conditional entropies, but not the zeros in GI-3 since they hold per class).
