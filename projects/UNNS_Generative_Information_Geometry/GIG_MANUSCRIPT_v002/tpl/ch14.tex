\chapter{Principal mathematical results, discoveries and implications}\label{ch:synthesis}

\section{The results in order of dependence}\label{sec:results}
The monograph established the following, with the status of each recorded in its theorem title (\cref{app:status}).

\paragraph{1. Admissibility is prefix-defined, and arithmetic admissibility is a finite lattice.} For SELECT-II, ordering $x$ is admissible iff $D(I)\mid m$ for every prefix set $I$ (\cref{lem:F}). Consequently the admissible set depends on the source only through finitely many valuation classes (\cref{prop:finite-lattice}), is closed under $\lcm$ of sources (\cref{thm:closure}), factorises over coprime parts (\cref{thm:block}), has the cube-product property (\cref{cor:CP}), and every face $F$ of the permutohedron enters the history complex at an explicit divisibility threshold $K_F$ (\cref{thm:face-threshold}). This is the exact link between number theory and topology.

\paragraph{2. The swap graph with its colours is the history complex.} The coloured swap graph determines $K_A$ up to isomorphism (\cref{thm:swap-reconstruction}); the uncoloured graph determines the $2$-skeleton, hence $\beta_0$ and $\beta_1$, in the prefix-defined case for $k\le8$ (\cref{thm:skeleton}), and leaves $\beta_2$ determined up to the rank of the third boundary map (\cref{thm:third-boundary}): two graph-isomorphic realizable systems with different $\beta_2$ would have to differ in that rank, and whether such a pair exists is not settled here.

\paragraph{3. Holes exist, and divisibility decides whether they survive.} A hole is a pair $(z,\varphi)$ of a cycle and a cocycle (\cref{lem:certificate}). The two anchor contents carry a $96$-face $2$-cycle whose survival is governed by an exact criterion in the activated gate primes (\cref{thm:anchor,thm:exact-survival}); the naive condition that a gate must be activated is false (\cref{prop:gate-nec}, \cref{prop:CRT}). For every cycle the set of sources at which it survives is described by minimal filling families (\cref{thm:AT1}), and a finite atlas classifies cycles by their laws (\cref{obs:atlas}).

\paragraph{4. Holes lift in dimension.} The lift operator satisfies $\partial\Lambda(f)=\Lambda(\partial f)+\first(f)+\last(f)$ (\cref{lem:LT0}); every filling gives a lifted cycle of one dimension higher (\cref{thm:lift-cycle}), the lifted class depends on the filling exactly through the first-facet complex (\cref{thm:filling-dependence,thm:JX}), and a certified four-dimensional hole exists (\cref{prop:H3-examples}).

\paragraph{5. Counting histories and recovering topology are different things.} The observation ladder (\cref{thm:obs-ladder}), the accessibility profile (\cref{thm:profile}) and the radius-two identity $|B_2(w)|=1+d+s-q$ (\cref{thm:radius2}) fix exactly what local data contain. Equal counts, equal radius-one data and even equal radius-two moments are compatible with different topology (\cref{prop:witnesses,thm:no-radius1,obs:S2-falsified}); on the six-label census radius-two histograms nevertheless separate (\cref{obs:residual}), an empirical fact whose cause is unknown and which does not predict out of sample (\cref{obs:oos}).

\paragraph{6. The forbidden histories carry the topology.} Alexander duality gives $\Hh_j(K_A)\cong\Hh^{k-3-j}(Y_A)$ (\cref{thm:AD}); for prefix-defined systems $Y_A\simeq N[D_A]$ (\cref{thm:nerve}), so $\beta_2(K_A)=\dim H_1(N[D_A])$, and the strong-collapse core is a complete invariant (\cref{cor:core-suffices}) while no tested count of the nerve is (\cref{obs:collapse}).

\paragraph{7. Locality of the constraint, not divisibility, is the dividing line.} Pairwise (consecutive-pair) grammars on six labels never produce $\beta_2>0$ (\cref{thm:pairwise}); consecutive-triple constraints do (\cref{prop:triple}); so do prefix-set constraints (\cref{obs:locality}).

\section{What the Substrate gains}\label{sec:implications}
Within the UNNS Substrate the results say that generated occurrences have a geometry, and that the geometry is controlled by arithmetic in a way that can be written down. The multiplicity of histories (the Hartley ambiguity of a state) and their topology are separate invariants of the same recursion: the first is a count, the second a property of how histories can be re-routed into one another by legal swaps. The prefix law makes this geometry finite and computable, the survival laws say which sources preserve a given obstruction, and the duality says that the same information can be read off the histories that are \emph{not} allowed. These are statements about the SELECT-II grammar and the prefix-defined and symbolic grammars treated; nothing here is claimed for other UNNS operators.

\section{Relation to established mathematics}\label{sec:relations}
Each layer has a classical neighbour, listed so the contribution of the monograph can be read against it.
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.4em,nosep]
\item[Shannon and Hartley.] The ambiguity $\log_2|F_t|$ is Hartley's measure of the set of complete histories and $\Ent(M\mid T)$ is Shannon's \cite{Hartley28,Shannon48,CoverThomas}; \cref{thm:hierarchy} is an instance of the classical inequality between them. No new entropy is defined. The separation results are negative statements about one-number summaries: the \emph{distribution} on $A$ is an incomplete description of the \emph{support} $A$. This complements, and says nothing against, Shannon theory.
\item[Alexander duality, nerves, strong homotopy.] \cref{thm:AD} is Alexander duality for a subcomplex of a sphere \cite{Hatcher,Munkres}; \cref{thm:nerve} is the nerve theorem \cite{Borsuk,Bjorner} with Quillen's closure lemma \cite{Quillen}; \cref{cor:core-suffices} uses strong collapses \cite{BarmakMinian} as a black box. What is specific is the dictionary identifying history complexes with complements of forbidden-prefix complexes, and the star-intersection lemma that makes the cover acyclic.
\item[Combinatorial commutative algebra.] \cref{thm:face-threshold} labels every face of the permutohedron by the $\lcm$ of its vertex labels, which is the labelling convention of Bayer and Sturmfels \cite{BayerSturmfels,MillerSturmfels}. Their criterion would read: the labelled permutohedron supports a cellular resolution of the monomial module generated by the vertex labels iff every $X_C(m)$ is acyclic. The anchor holes are then obstructions to such a resolution. This is an interpretation resting on a cited theorem; the module, its resolution and its Betti numbers have not been computed, and no later result depends on it.
\item[Persistent homology.] The family $m\mapsto X_C(m)$ is a filtration indexed by the divisibility lattice, so a multi-parameter persistence module in general \cite{ELZ02,Carlsson09,CarlssonZomorodian}. Births are the thresholds of \cref{thm:face-threshold}, and the change of Betti numbers across an inclusion is the rank--nullity identity behind every persistence computation. No barcode or multi-parameter invariant is computed; the atlas classifies cycles by survival laws, and its relation to the rank invariant is not established.
\item[Polytope reconstruction.] A simple polytope is determined by its graph \cite{BlindManiLevitska,Kalai88,Ziegler}. \cref{thm:swap-reconstruction} concerns instead an arbitrary induced subgraph of the permutohedron graph, for which reconstruction needs the swap colours, whose role is shown by \cref{prop:uncoloured-fails}; the proof uses only cardinalities of parabolic subgroups \cite{BjornerBrenti}.
\item[Symbolic dynamics.] A pairwise grammar is a one-step shift of finite type \cite{LindMarcus} restricted to words that are permutations. Entropy rate and zeta functions do not apply to fixed finite sets of words; what is studied is the topology of the finite set, and the locality line of \cref{obs:locality}.
\end{description}

\section{Scope}\label{sec:scope}
The proofs are exact; the arithmetic statements are finite or bounded and say so in their titles. The general claims are the chain-level calculus, the prefix law and its consequences, the reconstruction and duality theorems, and the radius-two identity. Everything that names a content, a source or a census is a finite computation, with the range stated. Specifically, the pairwise theorem is for six labels, the survival criterion is proved for the anchor classes and as a law for every cycle but is not an effective algorithm in general, and observation results on the census carry no claim beyond the census. The unproved items and the computations that were not repeated are listed in \cref{app:status}.
