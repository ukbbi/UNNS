# Two self-contained results (statements, proofs, certificates) — GIG-INFO v006

Setting. k=6 labels; P = proper nonempty subsets of [6]; histories = permutations; for a set A of histories, K_A = full subcomplex of the permutohedron P_6 on the vertex set A; Y_A = subcomplex of the order complex T=Δ(P) (≅S⁴) generated by the full flags (= prefix chains) of the histories NOT in A. Coefficients GF(2).
Classical inputs (not claimed as new): Alexander duality; the nerve theorem; Mayer–Vietoris spectral sequence of a cover; Quillen's closure-operator lemma. **A prior-art review has not been performed**; the novelty of the specific statements below is unassessed.

## Result I (dead-set nerve theorem)
*Setup.* A system is **prefix-defined** if there is a family 𝓐 ⊆ P such that a history is admissible iff all its prefix sets lie in 𝓐. (SELECT-II arithmetic systems are: ordering admissible ⇔ for every prefix set I, m·∏_{ℓ∈I}(ℓ+1)²/ℓ ∈ ℤ.) A set I∈P is **dead** if every flag through I is non-admissible; D = set of dead sets.
*Statement.* For prefix-defined A (proper, nonempty): β₂(K_A) = dim H₁(N[D];F₂), where N[D] is the simplicial complex on D whose simplices are the subsets with a common comparable element z∈P (⊆ or ⊇ each member). More generally H̃_j(K_A) ≅ H̃^{3−j}(N[D]).
*Proof outline.* (1) T-AD (Alexander duality for K_A ⊂ ∂P_6≅S⁴ ⇒ K_A ≃ S⁴∖|Y_A|): H̃_j(K_A) ≅ H̃^{3−j}(Y_A). (2) Y_A = ∪_{I∈D} St(I). (3) ∩_{I∈𝒮} St(I) = Δ(Q_𝒮) with Q_𝒮={x∈P comparable to all members}; Lemma L1: Q_𝒮 is empty or Δ(Q_𝒮) is contractible (closure operator x↦x∪A or x∪V; proof in v005 THEORY §1). (4) Nerve theorem ⇒ Y_A ≃ N[D].
*Finite certificates (independent of the engines):* `VERIFY/standalone_verify.py` — recomputes dead sets from the arithmetic definition; for 4 systems checks β₂ by the definition of K_A against H₁(N[D]); for all 290 known positives verifies a cycle z and an edge cocycle c (c(∂τ)=0 for all triangles τ of N[D], c(z)=1; `CERTIFICATES/positives_cycle_cocycle.json`).

## Result II (pairwise-adjacency theorem and its sharpness)
*Statement.* If A is the set of Hamiltonian paths of an arbitrary digraph G on six distinguishable events (admissibility = every consecutive pair allowed), A≠∅, then β₂(K_A)=0. The bound is sharp in the locality order: forbidding exactly the consecutive triples (1,2,0) and (5,4,3) gives Betti numbers (1,2,5,0,0,0).
*Proof outline.* Y_A=∪_{p∈B}Z_p over the bad ordered pairs B, where Z_p is generated by flags with adjacency p; a chain (ordered partition with block index b) lies in Z_{(x,y)} iff b(y)−b(x)∈{0,1}. In the Čech/Mayer–Vietoris spectral sequence only E_{0,1} and E_{1,0} enter H₁(Y_A). Finite exact facts: Z_p is H̃₀=H̃₁=0 for all 30 ordered pairs; Z_p∩Z_q is nonempty and connected for all 435 pairs; Z_p∩Z_q∩Z_r is nonempty for all 4060 triples. Hence E¹_{0,1}=0 and E²_{1,0}=H₁(2-skeleton of a simplex)=0, so H₁(Y_A)=0, and T-AD gives β₂(K_A)=0. Sharpness mechanism: Z₁,Z₂ (the two triples) have H̃₀=H̃₁=0 and Z₁∩Z₂ has six components, so H₁(Z₁∪Z₂)≅F₂⁵.
*Finite certificates:* `standalone_verify.py [ADJ]` (all 30/435/4060 checks, from the definition of Z_p as ordered-partition conditions, with closure under coarsening asserted) and `[3ADJ]` (β₂=5 from the definition of K_A; N=674).
Scope: six events; generalisation to other k is not claimed.
