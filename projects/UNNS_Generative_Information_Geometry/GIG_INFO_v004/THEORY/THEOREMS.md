## A. Structure
**T-AD [PROVED] Alexander duality for history complexes.** Let A be a proper non-empty subset of the vertex set of P_k and Y_A the simplicial complex, on the proper non-empty subsets of {0..k-1}, generated by the chains of the NON-admissible histories (the prefix sets of each ordering not in A). Then reduced H_j(K_A;F_2) ~ reduced H^{k-3-j}(Y_A;F_2). For k=6:  beta2(K_A) = dim H_1(Y_A);  beta1(K_A) = dim reduced H_2(Y_A);  beta3(K_A) = (number of components of Y_A) - 1.
Proof. Faces of P_k are ordered set partitions (B_1|...|B_j) and correspond bijectively to chains B_1 < B_1uB_2 < ... of proper subsets, i.e. to simplices c of the order complex T of the proper part of the Boolean lattice (a simplicial (k-2)-sphere); dim of the face = (k-2) - dim c, vertices of P_k = facets (maximal chains) of T, and the vertices of the face F(c) are the facets containing c. Hence F(c) in K_A iff every facet containing c lies in A iff c is not a face of a facet outside A iff c not in Y_A. For A != all (so Y_A non-empty and the single-block face is excluded) K_A is therefore the subcomplex of the dual cell decomposition of |T| formed by the cells dual to the simplices not in Y_A. The complement |T| minus |Y_A| deformation retracts onto this complex (standard: complement of a subcomplex retracts onto the full subcomplex of the barycentric subdivision on the barycentres of the simplices not in Y; Munkres, Elements of Algebraic Topology, Lemma 70.1). Alexander duality in S^{k-2} gives reduced H_j(S^{k-2} - |Y|) = reduced H^{k-3-j}(Y); over a field cohomology and homology have equal dimension. QED.
Machine check (A10): reduced b_j(K_A) = reduced b_{3-j}(Y_A) for j=0..3 and b_4(K_A)=0 on 700 systems (all 158 census positives, 342 census negatives, 200 arbitrary random vertex sets), 0 mismatches; beta2(K)=dim H_1(Y) in all 700.
Consequence: the topology of the history complex is the topology of the forbidden complex. beta2>0 <=> Y_A contains a 1-cycle that does not bound inside Y_A. For prefix-defined systems (admissibility = every prefix set lies in a family F) Y_A is the union of the closed stars of the forbidden subsets I not in F, each star a contractible ball. This reformulation explains WHERE beta2 comes from; it does not by itself explain why the local sums Q and S2 detect it.
