# GIG_I_v005_S2 — Arithmetic H₂ Obstruction Atlas: report
1. **Answer to the central question.** The 106 previously unexplained β₂ regimes (and all 158) do **not** require fundamentally different machinery: every one of them is, exactly, "a 2-cycle z of P_6 with P_z ⊆ A(m) and none of its minimal filling families ⊆ A(m)" — the Stage-1 architecture, with different cycles. The cycles and families differ, but the mechanism type is the same.
2. **Atlas.** 160 H₂-basis cycles (157 regimes with β₂=1, one with β₂=3) → 70 canonical (S₆) cycles → 23 law classes (canonical (P_z, minimal families)), 32 after adding the held-out failures. Every class has 4, 6 or 8 minimal families; classes have 0, 1 or 2 singleton gates (single prefix sets whose admission always kills the hole). Families computed by exact dualisation (q up to 33, impossible to enumerate).
3. **Coverage of the census.** All 158 regimes covered by ≥ 1 class, all 106 non-PS-1 ones included; 22 are covered by exactly one class, 136 by several; greedy cover = 9 classes. The Stage-1 class alone covers 59 regimes (the 52 of v004 + 7 more, because the exact criterion is weaker than "all gates closed"); 99 of the 106 need other classes.
4. **Predictive test (bounded).** Atlas v1: 18 false negatives of 211 hole regimes on fresh contents; atlas v2 (+ those): 0 false negatives of 171 on a fresh set, then 3 of 292 (1%) on a third. Zero false positives in all rounds (as AT-1 requires). The atlas is **not** closed.
5. **Theorems.** AT-1 survival law per cycle (finite-exact, any content), AT-2 existence criterion at m = K_{P_z} (proved). No analogue of Stage-1's arithmetic-realisability result was attempted for the new classes.
6. **Not shown.** Representative-independence of the classes; finiteness of the atlas; closed-form descriptions of the cycles; arithmetic realisability of the other classes' families; H₃ (Stage 3); T20d.
