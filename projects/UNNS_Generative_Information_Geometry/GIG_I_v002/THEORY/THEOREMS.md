| **GIG-T11** | *Cubical admissibility factorization.* For a source `m`, a word with `d` disjoint adjacent pairs `{a_i,b_i}` (`a_i≠b_i`) that may be exchanged independently, and all other letters fixed: `Adm(m)∩Q_d = G×E_1×…×E_d`, `G∈{0,1}`, `E_i⊆{0,1}` determined by the *formal* entry states. Hence `|Adm∩Q_d|∈{0}∪{2^j}` and the slice is a coordinate sub-cube. For `d=3`: counts `0,1,2,4,8`; **a six-vertex slice (Petrie hexagon, or a domino) is impossible.** Corollaries: *square closure* (three vertices of a commuting square ⇒ the fourth); every realizable set has CP; any realizable set containing the six vertices of a Petrie cycle contains its whole cube. | [PROVED] + [FINITE-EXACT] (all 1,170 commuting cubes of dim ≥2 in `P_6`, all realizable sets for contents ⊂ {1..12}) |
| **GIG-T12** | *Threshold-closure characterization.* `S=A_C(m)` for some `m` ⇔ `cl_K(S)=S`. Then `L(S)` is the unique divisibility-minimal (hence numerically smallest) realizing source; `cl_K` is a closure operator (extensive, monotone, idempotent), `cl_K(S)` is the smallest realizable superset of `S`; the realizable sets are closed under intersection (`A(m)∩A(m')=A(gcd)`); a closed `S` is realizable by a squarefree source iff `L(S)` is squarefree. | [PROVED] + independent checks |
| **GIG-T13** | *Reconstruction of the permutohedral history complex from the generator-coloured swap graph.* For **any** `A⊆S_k` (no arithmetic hypothesis): faces of `K_A` = pairs `(J,Z)`, `Z` a connected component of the `J`-coloured subgraph of `G_A` with `|Z|=|W_J|`; incidence = vertex-set inclusion with `|J|` dropping by 1. Hence `f`-vector, boundary matrices and `β_j` are functions of `(G_A, colouring, k)`. A colour-preserving isomorphism `G_A→G_B`, *up to a Coxeter-diagram automorphism*, induces an isomorphism of the face posets and the chain complexes, hence equal Betti numbers. | [PROVED] + [FINITE-EXACT] |
| **GIG-T14** | *Graph isomorphism ⇒ equality of every graph-derived accessibility profile.* If `G_A≅G_B` then the component-size multisets, the multisets of ball-size vectors `(|B_0(w)|,…,|B_r(w)|)` for every `r`, `P_r`, `I_r`, class entropy and the separating radius agree. | [PROVED] (+ tested) |
| **GIG-T15** *(new, from the v002 analysis)* | *Graph-only recoverability of the 2-skeleton under arithmetic admissibility.* Let `A` have CP and `k≤8`. Then the 2-faces of `K_A` are exactly the 4-cycles of the **uncoloured** `G_A` (squares) and the chordless 6-cycles of `G_A` that have no outside vertex adjacent to two of their vertices (braid hexagons). Consequently `f_2`, `β_0` and `β_1` of every arithmetically realizable piece (any content, any source) are functions of the isomorphism class of the uncoloured swap graph, and the colouring is not needed for `H_0,H_1`. | [PROVED] given the 6-cycle classification ([FINITE-EXACT] for `k≤8`); verified on every realizable set of the k=6 domains |
