# GIG-I v001 — Open problems and falsifiable conjectures

**GIG-C1 (finite-exact support, OPEN).** In the arithmetic census of distinct-label pieces, the *fine* accessibility signature through radius 3 determines the permutohedral Betti vector (k ≤ 5). Status: no counterexample in k=4 (labels ≤ 70) or k=5 (labels ≤ 26); radius 2 provably fails (T9, W5/W6). Falsifier: two realizable sets with equal radius-3 fine signatures and different Betti vectors (larger label bounds, or k=6).

**GIG-C2 (OPEN).** The minimal separating radius is unbounded in `k`. Mechanism suggested by T8: cells of dimension `j` in `K_A` have boundaries of increasing girth in `P_k`; their presence is first visible at larger radius. Observed minimum radius: k=4 → 1, k=5 → 3 (census).

**Q-hex (OPEN).** Which 6-cycles of an induced permutohedron graph are not hexagon faces (e.g. Petrie hexagons of 3-cubes, first available at k=6), and are the corresponding vertex sets arithmetically realizable? Such a set has the same graph as a filled hexagon but a nontrivial loop — a *graph-isomorphic* information-equivalent pair (stronger than T9).

**Q-void-sf (OPEN).** Do complete-history fibres (squarefree sources) contain a 2-void? None among k=5 realizable sets with labels ≤ 26 (the 15 voids found there all have non-squarefree sources only). Larger k / labels are untested.

**Q-rate (OPEN).** Growth law of `A(72^r)`: lower bound ≈ `2r` bits (T5), observed increments 4.6 → 7.1 bits for r = 2..10 and still rising with shrinking steps. Does `A(72^r)/r` converge? Is `|F_{72^r}|` asymptotically exponential in `r` or faster?

**Q-ensemble (OPEN).** Natural nonuniform ensembles on `F_t` (e.g. maximum-entropy given length distribution, multiplicative weights from labels) and the resulting `H(W|T=t)` versus `A(t)`.

**Q-source (OPEN).** Relationship between `A(t)` (complete-history), `A(t | m0)` (fixed-source) and the source-entropy `H(M|T=t)`: exact bounds beyond T2/T3.

**Q-repeat (OPEN, definitional).** A history complex for contents with repeated labels (inherited definitions cover distinct labels). Without it, homology claims are restricted to distinct-label pieces; the swap-graph information theory (T1–T8) holds for all pieces.

**Q-FIELD (out of scope for v001).** Information transitions under FIELD-I collective evolution; thermodynamic, quantum, continuous-curvature and non-orientable formulations. Not imported into v001 without a defined mathematical connection.
