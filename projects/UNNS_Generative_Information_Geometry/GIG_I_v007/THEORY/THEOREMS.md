# GIG-I v007 — The filling-dependence map J_X (theorem register)
Tags: [PROVED] · [FINITE-EXACT] · [BOUNDED] · [OPEN]. GF(2) throughout. Notation: P_k permutohedron (k labels), new rank r=k, first(f)=({r}|f), last(f)=(f|{r}); K_k(A) faces with prefix sets in A; X=K_{k+1}(A).
Definitions (v006 assessment, adopted): V_X={d∈Z_j(P_k): first(d)∈C_j(X)}, J_X:V_X→H_j(X), d↦[first(d)], r_fill(X)=dim im J_X.

- **J-1 [PROVED] Facet theorem.** first and last are injective, dimension-preserving chain maps P_k→P_{k+1} (∂first=first∂, ∂last=last∂, because the block {r} is a singleton and boundary only splits blocks). Pref(first(f))={{r}}∪{I∪{r}:I∈Pref(f)}. Hence F_X:={f: first(f)∈X}=K_k(A′), A′={I: I∪{r}∈A} (empty unless {r}∈A), V_X=Z_j(F_X), J_X factors through H_j(F_X) and equals first_*:H_j(F_X)→H_j(X). So **r_fill(X) ≤ β_j(F_X)**, and ker J_X ⊇ B_j(F_X). Same for last with A″.
- **J-2 [PROVED] Retraction criterion.** ρ(g)=g∖{r} if {r} is a singleton block of g, else 0, is a dimension-preserving chain map P_{k+1}→P_k over GF(2) (if r is not a singleton the two splits ({r}|U),(U|{r}) of its block delete to the same face and cancel), with ρ∘first=id. Consequently d∈ker J_X ⇒ d=∂ρ(w) for some admitted w, i.e. d bounds in the chain group on G_X=ρ(X_sing). This gives the lower bound r_fill ≥ dim Z_j(F_X) − dim(Z_j(F_X)∩∂C_{j+1}(G_X)). If ρ(X)⊆F_X (condition RET) then first_* is split injective and r_fill=β_j(F_X).
- **J-3 [FINITE-EXACT] Computation over 310 surviving lift instances** (202 E30 seed contents × 12 off-diagonal anchor patterns, X at m=K_P): r_fill∈{0,1}; r_fill=β_3(F_X) in all 310 (298 with 0, 12 with 1); the J-2 lower bound equals r_fill and β in all 310 (the sandwich is tight); RET fails in all 310, so injectivity of first_* is not explained by a global retraction — the sandwich is what proves it instance by instance. dim V_X=37 in all 12 instances with r_fill=1.
- **J-4 [FINITE-EXACT] Witnesses.** For (2,3,8,9,14,15,20) m=33600 and (3,8,9,14,15,20,21) m=793800: dim V_X=37, r_fill=1, dim ker=36, so exactly 2¹ distinct lifted classes are attainable by varying the first filling.
