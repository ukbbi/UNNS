# Pilot Questions

## Q1 — Structural discrimination

Can a multiscale UNNS representation distinguish real forced-isotropic
turbulence from matched controls that preserve selected conventional statistics?

This is the first gate.

## Q2 — Recurring families

Do independent turbulent cutouts repeatedly fall into a limited set of
structural/admissibility families?

Measure:
- family occupancy;
- within-family dispersion;
- between-family separation;
- time and location stability;
- sensitivity to filter scales and thresholds.

## Q3 — Persistence across scale

Do coherent structures preserve identifiable relations as the observation scale
changes?

Measure:
- persistence depth;
- connected-region continuity;
- splitting and merging;
- fragmentation rate;
- topology changes;
- cross-scale matching stability.

## Q4 — Restricted route grammar

Are parent->daughter structural transitions across adjacent scales strongly
non-uniform relative to matched controls?

Possible evidence:
- sparse transition matrix;
- low transition entropy;
- recurring high-probability routes;
- stable forbidden/rare transitions;
- improved next-state prediction over control baselines.

## Q5 — Intermittency

Are intermittent high-enstrophy / high-dissipation events associated with
specific structural families or route transitions rather than merely extreme
scalar amplitudes?

## Q6 — Spectrum independence

Does any detected UNNS organization survive a test against controls constructed
to preserve the radial energy spectrum?

A positive answer is especially important because reproducing known spectral
scaling alone is not sufficient evidence of a new structural description.

## Decision rule

Do not move from "structural discrimination" to claims about "families", or from
"families" to "grammar", unless the preceding question is supported by matched,
reproducible controls.
