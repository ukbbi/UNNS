Discrete time quasi-crystals in Rydberg atomic chain
Communications Physics 9, 141 (2026)
DOI: 10.1038/s42005-026-02572-0

Source-defined physical facts used only to interpret the diagnostic results:

- The model couples left and right DTC subsystems.
- The two external driving frequencies obey
  f_R / f_L = (1 + sqrt(5)) / 2.
- Response components are indexed by integer combinations of the two drive frequencies with subharmonic order n >= 2; the paper focuses on n=2.
- For the entanglement-entropy comparison:
    low:  f_L = 1.00
    DTQC: f_L = 2.00
    high: f_L = 3.34
- At high driving frequency the two subsystems progressively decouple.
- At low frequency the response becomes aperiodic/chaotic.
- The intermediate regime is identified by the authors as the proper DTQC range.
