# Current interpretation

## What the results establish

1. Complete STRUC-I curves define reproducible galaxy-specific structural fingerprints.
2. The fingerprints form a non-random and partly low-dimensional atlas.
3. Major atlas coordinates align with ordinary physical galaxy properties.
4. The atlas also records point count, gap structure, and radial coverage.
5. Weak Persistence forms a pronounced response regime.
6. Stable persistence and full connectivity are distinct.
7. Fingerprint expression changes under sampling and baryonic-preparation changes.
8. The tested static global gravity model does not convert fingerprint identity into a consistent held-out gain.
9. ρ is the leading channel for a new local or regime-conditioned transfer experiment.

## Preferred conceptual object

Use:

> **galaxy-specific multiscale structural fingerprint**

The fingerprint is expressed through a measured ladder. Its dependence on sampling, coverage, and preparation is part of the measurement geometry and can be explicitly modelled.

## Scientific consequence

The project has created a new structural coordinate system for galaxy rotation-curve data.

The next advance is to normalize the representation layer, map uncertainty regions, and identify which structural relations persist across measurement transformations.
