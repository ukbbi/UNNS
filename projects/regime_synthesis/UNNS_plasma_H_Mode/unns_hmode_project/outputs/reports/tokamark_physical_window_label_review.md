## Manual labeling instructions

1. Open each preview CSV and inspect physical diagnostics first: D-alpha, soft-X, profile gradients, power, and density.
2. Assign labels in `tokamark_physical_window_label_template.csv` only after physical review.
3. Keep `excluded_from_validation = true` until the label is physically justified.
4. Use `AMBIGUOUS` instead of forcing a label.
5. Use `UNLABELABLE` when the physical evidence is insufficient.
6. Only after labels are assigned, compare them against `m_edge_conf(t)`.
