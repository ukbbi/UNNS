**pair order alone is not enough**

and

**localization alone is not enough**.

## 6. What is genuinely new here

Quantum typicality is the first coordinate in this branch that is not merely a
property of a time series or a classical state-space orbit.

It probes the response of highly scrambled, entangled states that sample the
many-body Hilbert space.

An ordinary classical periodic attractor has no directly equivalent
Hilbert-space typicality coordinate. The classical control is therefore
recorded as **N/A**, not artificially assigned zero.

**temporal closure + experimentally measured many-body spectral order

The next theoretical step, if pursued, should formalize the quantum many-body
sector itself rather than further modifying C(q).
