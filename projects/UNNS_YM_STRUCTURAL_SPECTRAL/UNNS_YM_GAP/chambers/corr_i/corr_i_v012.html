WHAT CORR-I TESTS
CORR-I operates directly on Euclidean correlator sequences rather than on a sorted mass ladder. It tests positivity-compatible correlator geometry (monotonicity, log-convexity, Hausdorff/Hankel moment positivity), effective-mass persistence, and a controlled near-zero-energy adversary. The chamber records whether a low-energy spectral component can be introduced without violating the supplied correlator data and structural constraints; it does not by itself prove a Yang–Mills mass gap.
