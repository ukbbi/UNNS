## 1. Project origin

This project is born directly from the completed and closed research project:

`UNNS_YM_GAP`

Full former title:

**UNNS Yang–Mills Gap / Vacuum Boundary Non-Crossability**

That project must remain closed and historically unchanged.

> **Gapless Yang–Mills continuation is structurally non-generable under refinement, whereas the massive branch is refinement-stable.**

Its stronger structural interpretation was:

> **The Yang–Mills vacuum is separated from every admissible non-vacuum structure by a refinement-stable non-crossable structural boundary.**

> **The proposed refinement-stable structural vacuum boundary was not established; the central Class B trajectory crossed the proposed boundary once, while uncertainty prevented definitive falsification.**

This statement must remain immutable.

The important consequence of the closed project is not merely that a proposed boundary failed.

The deeper structural implication is that the vacuum did **not** behave as an exterior object lying outside UNNS admissibility.

The structural trajectory remained part of the same admissible representational space while approaching — and in the central Class B reconstruction crossing — the proposed vacuum boundary.

This motivates the new foundational proposition:

\[
\boxed{\textbf{Vacuum belongs to the UNNS Substrate.}}
\]

Old structural picture:

\[

\text{vacuum}
\]

New structural picture to investigate:

\[

\rightarrow
\text{vacuum-like structural regime}

V

with the entire progression occurring inside the same UNNS structural domain.

## 3. Core new hypothesis

### H1 — Structural Vacuum Continuity

> **The Yang–Mills vacuum is not separated from admissible non-vacuum structure by a universal refinement-stable structural boundary.**

Equivalent structural statement:

\[
\inf_{L\in\mathcal A_{\rm YM}\setminus\{V\}}
D_{\rm struct}(L,V)=0.
\]

Operationally:

\[
\forall \delta>0,\quad
\exists L\in\mathcal A_{\rm YM},\ L\neq V:

\]

This is a structural statement only.

It is **not** the claim that the physical Yang–Mills mass gap vanishes.

The most important conceptual proposition is:

In shorthand:

## 12. Strong falsification conditions for the new hypothesis

The new project must be capable of failing.

Structural vacuum continuity should be rejected if a sufficiently strong experiment shows that:

1. every admissible refinement path terminates before structural vacuum approach;
2. common refinement produces a positive lower bound
   \[
   D_{\rm struct}(L,V)\ge\delta>0;
   \]
3. apparent crossings disappear under independent representations;
4. crossing requires loss of admissibility;
5. the effect is produced only by digitization/noise/window choice;
6. continuity occurs only in synthetic controls but not measured-data representations;
7. an independent structural chamber reproduces universal non-crossability.

These falsification criteria should be formalized before Stage 2.
