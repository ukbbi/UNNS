## H1 — Structural Vacuum Continuity

Let \(\mathcal A_{\rm YM}\) be the admissible Yang–Mills-derived UNNS structural domain and \(V\) the vacuum structural object.

\[
\boxed{
V\in\overline{\mathcal A_{\rm YM}\setminus\{V\}}
}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\inf_{X\in\mathcal A_{\rm YM}\setminus\{V\}}
D_{\rm struct}(X,V)=0.
\]

Operationally, there must exist refinement-compatible admissible non-vacuum structures whose structural distance to the independently defined vacuum proxy tends toward zero without requiring chamber rescue rules or loss of admissibility.

## H0 — Refinement-Stable Structural Isolation

There exists a positive lower bound \(\delta_V>0\) such that sufficiently refined admissible non-vacuum structures satisfy

\[
D_{\rm struct}(X,V)\ge\delta_V.
\]

This is the structural-isolation alternative.

## Distinctions that remain frozen

Structural continuity, spectral discreteness, and the physical Yang–Mills mass gap are separate claims.

Neither

\[
D_{\rm struct}\to0
\]

nor a crossing of any structural coordinate implies

\[
\Delta_{\rm YM}=0.
\]

Likewise, a nonzero spectral scale does not by itself establish a positive UNNS structural separation.
