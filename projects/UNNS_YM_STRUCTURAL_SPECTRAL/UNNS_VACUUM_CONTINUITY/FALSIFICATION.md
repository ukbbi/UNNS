# FALSIFICATION.md

**Project:** `UNNS_VACUUM_CONTINUITY`  
**Status:** Frozen Stage-1 falsification logic

Structural Vacuum Continuity must be capable of failing.

## Isolation-supporting result

Within the preregistered representation class, H1 is rejected if sufficiently strong refinement establishes a positive lower bound

\[
D_{\rm struct}(X,V)\ge\delta>0
\]

for every admitted vacuum-directed trajectory and that separation survives uncertainty, common refinement, vacuum-proxy variation, and an independent structural representation.

## Additional falsification routes

Structural continuity is not supported if:

1. every admissible refinement path terminates before vacuum approach;
2. apparent approach disappears under common refinement;
3. apparent crossing requires loss of admissibility;
4. the effect disappears under an independent representation;
5. the effect is explained by digitization, noise, window choice, or an unfrozen normalization;
6. continuity is present only in synthetic controls and not in measured-data representations;
7. an independent structural chamber reproduces universal non-crossability.

## Indeterminate outcomes

Failure of a metric, vacuum proxy, or representation does not count as evidence for isolation. Such cases are classified as indeterminate and require a protocol-level diagnosis rather than reinterpretation of the result.
