# UNNS-SCALE — Research Charter

UNNS-SCALE is an independent, local research project. Its subject is how large integer domains of the UNNS Substrate relate to smaller nested ones: exact scale embeddings, cross-scale dynamics, structural preservation laws, and — only afterwards — computational consequences at trillion-scale domains.

## Principles
1. **Local development only.** Mathematics, chambers, code, results and verification live in this folder.
2. **Existing GitHub research is read-only reference material.** No commits, branches, issues or other changes to `ukbbi/UNNS`. Nothing here is pushed.
3. **Frozen UNNS foundations are preserved without modification.** Definitions are quoted and rebuilt locally (see PROVENANCE.md); no frozen file is edited, copied into this tree, or imported by code.
4. **Research freedom.** New grammars, operators and scaling constructions are developed wherever the mathematics justifies them.
5. **Publication only after approval.** GitHub publication happens only after a stable research release has been validated and approved.

## Ordering of objectives
1. Exact scale embeddings.
2. Cross-scale dynamics.
3. Structural preservation laws.
4. Only then: computational benefit at trillion scale (Gate 4, not started).

## Evidence discipline
Every claim carries one of: PROVED, CERTIFIED, EXPERIMENTAL, OPEN. Truncated-graph distances are upper bounds, never lower-bound certificates. Contraction of a representation is never reported as preservation of a target property (the frozen SCR spin-ice experiment is the standing warning: 16,777,216 → 2,604 admitted states, yet 10 of 26 target states retained).
