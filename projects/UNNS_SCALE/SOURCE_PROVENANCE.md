## Prior UNNS source records

The SELECT-II arithmetic graph used here is defined by edges `{kN, k(N+1)²}` for positive integers `k,N`. Historical read-only source provenance is preserved at [`releases/PROVENANCE.md`](releases/PROVENANCE.md), referencing the public [ukbbi/UNNS](https://github.com/ukbbi/UNNS) repository at commit `cc77e91af530b881b161bf3ff86a2fe0f9819c19`, including `projects/UNNS_ARITHMETIC_DYNAMICS` and the Scaling Lemma.

The UNNS-SCALE package **does not alter** or incorporate that upstream repository's frozen files. All chamber algorithms and proofs are the submitted independent local research records. This GitHub-ready ZIP was curated offline from the user-supplied `UNNS_SCALE.zip`; **no push, commit, branch, issue or other remote GitHub action was performed**.

## Integrity and corrections

Original release files remain byte-identical and retain their original SHA-256 manifests; later corrections are referenced in [CORRECTIONS.md](CORRECTIONS.md).
