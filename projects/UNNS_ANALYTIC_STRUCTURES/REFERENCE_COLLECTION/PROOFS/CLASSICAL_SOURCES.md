## ANS-I v009
- T. Estermann, On certain functions represented by Dirichlet series, Proc. London Math. Soc. (2) 27 (1928), 435-448. Used in T45a. The paper was not read; its statement was checked through web search results, including L. Delabarre, Bull. Soc. Math. France 141 (2013), 225-265 (arXiv:1001.3838), and G. Bhowmik and J.-C. Schlage-Puchta, Essential singularities of Euler products (arXiv:1001.1891).
- A. Selberg, On the zeros of Riemann's zeta-function, Skr. Norske Vid.-Akad. Oslo I (1942), no. 10. Positive proportion of zeros on the critical line (distinct zeros of odd order). Used in T38a; statement seen through web search results.
- H. Bohr and E. Landau (1914), zero-density estimate N(sigma, T) = O(T) for fixed sigma > 1/2. Used in T38a as a classical theorem; original not consulted.

## ANS-I v010

Search results also list 2026 preprints claiming larger proportions (e.g. arXiv:2609.02882); not assessed and not used.

- Background for T46c: the shifted divisor problem (A. E. Ingham; T. Estermann). Original papers not consulted.
- Weyl equidistribution and Bohr-Landau: classical.
