# Analytic Structures of the UNNS Substrate (ASUNNS)

UNNS Research Collective. Research record through **ANS-I v015**, **ANS-II v002** and **ANS-II-ND v003** (27 November 2026).

An analytic study of the histories generated by the integer nesting grammar

$$x \mapsto x\,\frac{(N+1)^2}{N},\qquad N \mid x,$$

and of extensions with further multipliers: their sources and endpoints, the history Dirichlet series $G_k(s)=\sum_{|w|=k}E(w)^{-s}$, convergence and singularities, generated populations, collective growth, and an entropy principle for admissibility. Each theorem states its grammar and hypotheses.

## Reading the status labels

- **Proof status** (PROVED, CONJECTURE, COMPUTED, CERTIFIED, OPEN) says whether a written argument or computation exists.
- **Review** says who has checked it. "UNNS Research Collective review" is the programme's own review. Entries marked *internal* in the release tables record work or checks done within the programme.
- **Nothing here has had external peer review**, and no literature novelty review has been completed.
- Finite computations support or test statements; they are not proofs. The certified computation ANS2N-T06 states its own conditions and has not been independently rerun.
- Statements M1 to M8 in the Cross-Structural Research Map are not yet reviewed and are not part of the theorem catalogue.
- No result implies or approaches a proof of the Riemann hypothesis, and no physical interpretation is claimed.

In the 130 catalogued entries: 122 reviewed, 4 conjectures, 1 certified computation, 1 computed result, 2 baseline notes.

## About this edition

- Frozen release archives and superseded working files are kept outside this repository. Some historical notes refer to them (`08_ARCHIVE/`, `.zip` names).
- Proof files are byte-identical to those in the release archives. Elsewhere (release tables, register, notes, program comments and the compendium's quoted statements) attribution wording is standardised for this edition, with review attributed to the UNNS Research Collective. The mathematics of statements and proofs is unchanged.
- The proof files and the typeset working notes keep their original wording, where "owner" refers to the same programme review.
- Per-release manifests are historical records. [`CHECKSUMS.sha256`](CHECKSUMS.sha256) is the integrity baseline for this edition: run `sha256sum -c CHECKSUMS.sha256` from this folder.
