# Research roadmap

| Stage | Target | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|
| A — Baseline | One-step degree, roots, endpoint incidence and inversion | Exact statements with proofs and independently checked examples |
| B — Depth | Minimal endpoints at depth 2 and beyond; exact valuation formula | Independent enumeration agreement; explicit coupling examples |
| C — Growth | Abscissa of convergence, summatory growth, depth weights | Rigorous upper/lower bounds; conditional results marked |
| D — Arithmetic | Multiplicativity tests, Euler product obstruction, prime-coordinate coupling | Exact identities or counterexamples; classical controls |
| E — Generalization | Other integral-image grammars and extended scaling sectors | Hypotheses and invariants separated by grammar |
| F — Synthesis | Analytic comparison, singularities and potential RH-sensitive quantities | Proof register, reproducible release and manuscript draft |

Stages are research targets, not completed findings. Do not advance a conjecture to theorem status without a proof.
