# Scope and foundations

The analytic investigation concerns admissibility-governed generative structures, including states, histories, minimal endpoints, ancestry, mergers and obstructions. The selected integer nesting grammar is the first explicit test sector, not an identification of the whole Substrate.

## Three levels
1. Classical arithmetic: divisor sums, Möbius inversion, Dirichlet series, Euler products and zeta functions.
2. Grammar-dependent generative structure: endpoint multiplicities, depth, ordering constraints, mergers, growth and analytic invariants.
3. Generalized sectors: rational integral-image grammars and further algebraic/topological constructions with explicitly defined analytic observables.

## Research questions
- Which analytic factors are universal consequences of divisibility persistence?
- Which analytic features distinguish grammars with different history architectures?
- How do prime coordinates interact through legal histories?
- What is the growth and singularity structure of minimal history endpoint multiplicity?
- Under what hypotheses can a grammar be reconstructed from analytic observables?

No hypothesis about physical realization or the Riemann Hypothesis is required for the initial investigation.
