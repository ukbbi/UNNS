\section{Verification}\label{app:ver}

All new mathematics of this paper is checked by the independent script \texttt{verify\_graph\_ent.py}, which uses exact integer and rational arithmetic, symbolic algebra (sympy), $50$-digit arithmetic (mpmath), and explicit edge matrices. The stages are summarised in Table~\ref{tab:ver}. Verification of the inherited releases is \textsc{not rerun}: the four inherited archives are present bit-identically (SHA-256 below) and only their recorded numerical rows are compared (stage G8).

{\small\input{../tables/verification_table.tex}}
\captionof{table}{Verifier stages. G1--G3: graph facts ($k,N$ edge labels, adjacency criterion, degree formula, triangle-freeness on $G[1,40000]$ and the exact equation $r_ar_b=r_c$, the $4$- and $5$-cycles, $F(x)$ structure for $x<2500$, the induced-subgraph theorem for $m\le150$ by the lowest-terms test). G4: characteristic polynomials (exact, sympy), numerical spectral radii, exact rational Collatz--Wielandt certificates ($m=2,\dots,40$, intervals of width $<10^{-9}$ containing $\lambda_m$), cubic bounds and series. G5: the trace formula, brute-force enumeration of NB walks, primitive-orbit integrality, the Ihara series identity, the cycle inventory. G6: the maximal-entropy chain (entropy rate $\log\lambda_m$ to $10^{-12}$) and the label-shift determinant. G7: forward walks and finite mechanisms. G8: comparison with the inherited rows. G9: general $x$ and window entropies. G10: archive hashes. G11--G15 (Version 1.2): stationary distribution and its constancy along paths, the subdominant modulus $\sqrt{m/\lambda_m}$, the window lower bound against computed entropies, the worked examples $F_2$, $F_3$, $F(12)$, and the comparison statements of Corollary~\ref{cor:theta}.}\label{tab:ver}



The theorem proofs are self-contained; the computations serve as independent checks. In particular the induced-subgraph theorem (Theorem~\ref{thm:induced}) is proved for all $m$ and was in addition confirmed by direct computation for $1\le m\le150$.

\section{Source provenance}\label{app:prov}
\paragraph{Where the sources are held.} The releases below are cited at the fixed commit \texttt{22d8c2e2\dots03808} of the collection~\cite{coll}, each by its release directory. The collection holds the release packages with their own manifests. \texttt{UNNS\_PRIME\_DYN} is not part of that collection; it is identified by archive name and SHA-256 only. The archive hashes in the bibliography are those recorded in the collection (and, for the four archives kept in \texttt{inherited/}, were also checked bit-for-bit by stage G10); the verifiers of all inherited releases are \textsc{not rerun}.

\paragraph{Source of each theorem family.} ``Inherited'' means that the statement was formulated in the named release; every such statement used here is proved in the text, and no proof relies on the release. ``This paper'' means that no cited release states the result.
