08Scope guards

Read each constant relative to its object.
  • Sharp distances 6, 5, 5 belong to the reversible graph G; girth 4 is attributed to U3-COR.
  • 2869/49 is the leading factor of the certified ADJ4 selected return; finite residue corrections come from a computer-assisted certificate (not independently refereed).
  • The entropy chain and gap-closure statements are exact for the family F(x)≅Θ(1,3,…,3), not general graph laws.
  • No identification with physical constants is made by these papers.
