\section{U3.1b promoted to a theorem}
\begin{thm}For every $L\ge1$ there is a lexicographically-first shortest path $w=(v_0,\dots,v_L)$ with exactly $M(w)=2^{L-1}$ compatible mark assignments (endpoints marked).\end{thm}
\emph{Proof.} $G$ is connected, infinite and locally finite, so the sphere of radius $L$ about any vertex is nonempty; take $w=P(a,b)$ with $d(a,b)=L$. A contiguous subpath of a lex-first shortest path is lex-first shortest between its endpoints: it is shortest, and a lexicographically smaller replacement would give a lexicographically smaller whole path of equal length. Hence every subset of the $L-1$ internal vertices cuts $w$ into canonical routing segments, giving $2^{L-1}$ compatible markings; there cannot be more since only internal positions are free. $\square$ (Computational check: subpath lemma and $M=2^{L-1}$ confirmed for $L\le5$, three geodesics per length, source $1$.) The ``proof sketch / $L\le5$ only'' qualification of v001 is removed.
\section{Girth correction}
v001 proved: $G$ is triangle-free and has odd girth $5$. The assessment text suggested $\mathrm{girth}(G)=5$; that is \textbf{false}. The cycle $2\!-\!8\!-\!36\!-\!9\!-\!2$ is a $4$-cycle (edges $\{2,8\},\{8,36\},\{36,9\},\{9,2\}$, each checked against the exact neighbour engine; it is the embedding already realized for $C_4$ in v001). Hence $\mathrm{girth}(G)=4$, and odd girth $=5$ by triangle-freeness plus the $5$-cycle $6,24,96,784,27$.
Self-contained triangle proof (checked symbolically): with $r_N=(N+1)^2/N$ a triangle requires $r_ar_b=r_c$ (other sign patterns are the same equation after renaming), i.e.\ $c(a+1)^2(b+1)^2=ab(c+1)^2$. So $abc$ is a square, $h=\sqrt{abc}$, $c(a+1)(b+1)=(c+1)h$, $c\mid h$, $h=ct$, $ab=ct^2$, $(a+1)(b+1)=t(c+1)$, and $a+b=(t-1)(1-ct)\le0$, contradiction.
\section{Entropy qualification}
The v001 sentence ``equality holds iff the roof is constant'' is restricted. Nonconstant roofs cohomologous to a constant can also give equality; the general framework is pressure and equilibrium measures. What is established: in the golden-mean two-symbol family with roof values $(2,d,d)$, topological entropy equals the Parry-lift (Abramov) value exactly when $d=2$ (6 of 56 embeddings; strict inequality in the other 50).
