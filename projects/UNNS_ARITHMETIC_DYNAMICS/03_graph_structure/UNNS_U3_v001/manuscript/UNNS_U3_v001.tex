\begin{abstract}
We ask which mathematical structures the reversible SELECT-II graph $G$ (vertices $\mathbb N_{>0}$, edges $\{kN,k(N+1)^2\}$) carries \emph{intrinsically}, which it preserves through \emph{faithful coding}, and which require \emph{additional generative information}. Results: (i) marked path histories represent every sequence over $\mathbb N_{>0}$ exactly (Proved), while unmarked walks are ambiguous, with ambiguity count exactly $2^{L-1}$ in the extremal case (Verified-bounded, $L\le5$; proof sketch given); (ii) discrete suspension over a subshift conjugates the first-return map to the shift, with entropy given measure-wise by Abramov's formula (Proved; numerically corroborated on 56 embeddings); (iii) $G$ is triangle-free and has odd girth exactly $5$ (Proved + certificate); (iv) the graph-intrinsic admissibility shifts $X_r(F)$ cannot encode the golden-mean shift (Proved), whereas full shifts are realizable (Proved). Nothing here modifies the frozen U0--U2, TI, TI-DYN or RN releases.
\end{abstract}

\section{U3-1: marked information}
\begin{defn}For a sequence $s$ the marked history $\iota(s)$ concatenates $P(s_n,s_{n+1})$ and marks the positions of the $s_n$.\end{defn}
\begin{thm}[U3.1a, Proved] $D(\iota(s))=s$ for every finite or infinite $s\in\mathbb N_{>0}^{*}$; hence $G$ represents every positive-integer sequence. (Marks are read off; $G$ is connected, so every $P(a,b)$ exists.)\end{thm}
\begin{prop}[U3.1b, unmarked ambiguity] Let $M(w)$ be the number of marked sequences with routing-compatible markings of the unmarked walk $w$. In the extremal case (concatenation of lex-first geodesics, whose subpaths are lex-first geodesics) $M(w)=2^{L-1}$ for $L$ segments. Verified for $L\le5$ (exhaustive extremal search) and on all $4^4$ words of a sample; hence $\lceil\log_2 M\rceil=L-1$ bits of marks are needed, unbounded in $L$. Example $[1,4,16]$: $M=2$.\end{prop}
Routing was validated against brute-force BFS for all pairs with $d\le5$, 500 decode trials, zero failures.
\section{U3-2: discrete suspension}
For a subshift $(X,\sigma)$ with embedding $e$ of the alphabet, roof $r(s)=d_G(e_{s_0},e_{s_1})$ ($2$ for repeats). The Kakutani tower has first-return map conjugate to $\sigma$ (checked on 200 random golden-mean sequences). For ergodic $\mu$, $h_{\mu^r}=h_\mu/\bar r$ (Abramov); topological entropy is the supremum over measures, hence $\ge$ the lift value of the Parry measure. \emph{Equality holds iff the roof is constant}: observed exactly for the six embeddings with $d=2$ (e.g.\ $(3,4),(5,8),(6,8)$), strict otherwise.
\textbf{Golden mean} ($\log\varphi$ over base): renewal equation $1-w-w^{d}=0$, $w=\lambda^{-2}$. Over embeddings $\{e_0,e_1\}\subset\{1..8\}$ (56 ordered pairs): period 2 in all cases; expanded-graph and renewal entropies agree to $1.5\times10^{-15}$; Parry-lift entropy equals the Abramov value to $1.7\times10^{-16}$; five distinct $d$ values / entropies. E.g.\ $(1,2)$: $d=5$, $h_{top}=0.14060$, $h_{lift}=0.13154$, $\bar r=3.6584$.
\textbf{Embedding dependence of mixing} (three-symbol full shift, 84 triples): period 2 for 53, period 1 for 31; $\{1,2,3\}$ has period 2, $\{6,96,784\}$ period 1. Hence mixing of the suspension is \emph{not} a property of the base system alone.
\section{U3-3: intrinsic admissibility (decisive test)}
$X_r(F)=\{s: d_G(s_n,s_{n+1})\le r\}$ on a finite vertex window $F$.
\begin{thm}[Obstruction, Proved] The pair relation is reflexive and symmetric; therefore every constant sequence is admissible and fixed points of $X_r(F)$ number $|F|$. The golden-mean shift has one fixed point, so it is not conjugate to any $X_r(F)$; no letter-to-letter intrinsic rule encodes it. (Verified: no violations in 60 random windows.)\end{thm}
\begin{thm}[Realizability, Proved] Full shifts are realized by cliques of $G^{(r)}$ (balls of radius $\lfloor r/2\rfloor$).\end{thm}
\begin{thm}[Triangle-free, Proved] $G$ contains no triangle; hence at $r=1$ no graph with a triangle is realizable. Proof: an edge $\{kN,k(N+1)^2\}$; a triangle forces integers $a,b,c,t$ with $a+b=(t-1)(1-ct)\le0<2\le a+b$, contradiction (identity checked symbolically; sign check $t,c<400$; no triangle among $x\le3000$).\end{thm}
\begin{prop}[Odd girth, Proved by certificate] The cycle $6,24,96,784,27$ has all five edges in $G$, so $G$ is non-bipartite with odd girth $5$.\end{prop}
Realizability of the 30 connected graphs on $\le5$ vertices (Registered observation, bounded search): $r=1$: 19 excluded by the triangle theorem, 10 realized, $K_{2,3}$ \emph{not found} in the universe $V\le10^6$, first vertex $\le300$; $r=2$ and $r=3$: 30/30 realized, all embeddings re-verified by exact distance. ``Not found'' is not an impossibility claim.
\section{U3-4/U3-5: compiled admissibility and cost ledger}
An explicit forbidden list (golden mean: 2 bits; no-11-22 over 3 symbols: 8; no-111: 3) can be compiled into a marked-history observer; this information is \emph{introduced}, not intrinsic, and is accounted separately from route cost $C_{route}$ and certification cost $C_{cert}$. Embedding (8 bits for a 16-wide window) and radius (3 bits) add further declared knowledge. Alphabet-embedding dependence of structure is shown in \S3. All neighbourhoods used were certified under the RN-0 policy (primality proofs only below $3.317\times10^{24}$).
