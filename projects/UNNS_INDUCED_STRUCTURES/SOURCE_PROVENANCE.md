## Independently generated external sources

- Integer sequences: native recurrence, primality, totient, Catalan, Sylvester definitions, with generated finite samples and scripts retained under `SIB_I_v001/data/` and subsequent releases. Fibonacci exact integer samples and rank-activation findings are under FUG-I.
- Maxwell: source-free Maxwell models, periodic dielectric models, modulation specifications and spectral controls are documented in MUG-I and CIB-I individual sources. Later Maxwell claims are source-locked to the stated medium, pump and solver.
- Quantum teleportation: Lago-Rivera et al., *Supporting data for Long distance multiplexed quantum teleportation from a telecom photon to a solid-state qubit* (Zenodo 2023), <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7680437>. Raw `.pt` events are **not** bundled. Source-format assumptions, file provenance and channel semantics must be verified against original files and publication before physical conclusions.
