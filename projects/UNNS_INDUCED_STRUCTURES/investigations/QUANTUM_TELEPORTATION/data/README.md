# Raw Quantum Teleportation Data — Source and Provenance

This directory records the **external experimental data** used in the quantum-teleportation branch (`QTI-I v001–v003`) of the `UNNS_INDUCED_STRUCTURES` research collection. The raw detections were generated by the original experimental authors, **not** by the UNNS project.

## Original dataset

**Lago-Rivera, Dario; Rakonjac, Jelena V.; Grandi, Samuele; de Riedmatten, Hugues (2023).** *Supporting data for “Long distance multiplexed quantum teleportation from a telecom photon to a solid-state qubit”.* Zenodo, version 1, published 27 February 2023.

- **Permanent DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7680437
- **Dataset record:** https://zenodo.org/records/7680437
- **Related preprint:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.06249

The Zenodo record supplies raw detection archives for the short-distance experiment, the 1 km optical-fibre experiment, and the repetition-rate experiment.

## Relationship to the UNNS research

The `QTI-I v001–v003` investigations analyze timestamp-based structural features and channel-conditioned relationships in the original experimental data. Their computational outputs, interpretations, tests, and limitations are documented in the corresponding frozen QTI-I releases. The research does **not** claim that the original experiment was designed to test UNNS, or that the detected relationships establish a uniquely UNNS-specific quantum mechanism.

The experiment's physical interpretation and the provenance of its raw detector records remain those of the original authors. Readers should cite the Zenodo dataset and associated publication **in addition to** any UNNS analysis they use.

## Storage and reproducibility

These large original ZIP archives are **not part of the small Git-tracked reference collection**, its `CHECKSUMS.sha256`, or its historical source-file ledger unless explicitly added in a separately documented revision. An ordinary GitHub commit cannot accept either of the two locally retained archives as a regular file because each exceeds GitHub's 100 MiB per-file limit. Obtain them through the permanent Zenodo record above, or distribute separately using an appropriate large-file mechanism.

To check downloaded copies against Zenodo's listed MD5 checksums in PowerShell:

```powershell
Get-FileHash -Algorithm MD5 -LiteralPath '.\1 km.zip'
Get-FileHash -Algorithm MD5 -LiteralPath '.\Short distance.zip'
```

To record independent local SHA-256 fingerprints for reproducibility:

```powershell
Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 -LiteralPath '.\1 km.zip'
Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256 -LiteralPath '.\Short distance.zip'
```

Do not edit, recompress, or rename the upstream raw archives without recording the transformation. Local SHA-256 values must be calculated from the actual files; none are asserted in this README.
