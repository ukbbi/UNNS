# Research continuation after FUG-I v004

## FUG-I Phase 5 candidate

**Unrestricted activation obstructions and a source-locked merger spectrum.**

A decisive next theorem would settle one of the following:

1. **All-label first-activation family:** prove that some infinite Fibonacci index family has unchanged *unrestricted* immediate incoming label set but gains new depth>=2 histories, or identify the number-theoretic obstruction preventing that form of theorem.
2. **Kernel onset classification:** for each selected nonpermutation multiplicative relation between nesting-label words, compute its minimal common endpoint E_pair, then rank z(E_pair); identify overlap and ordering of appearance schedules across kernels.
3. **Controlled discrimination:** devise matched size-/valuation-conditional controls before treating observed Fibonacci history differences as evidence of special substrate behavior.

Do not claim numerical census at arbitrarily high Fibonacci indices without a justified factorization regime; many F_n exceed 64-bit range. Use modular rank methods and isolated exact proofs instead.
