# Limits and inference controls

1. The UNNS primitive `M ⟂ N` includes rational non-anchor occurrences. FUG-I v004 investigates only its **selected integer self-substitution** continuation `T_N`; statements cannot be extended automatically to all rational nesting grammars.
2. The rank law follows from classical Fibonacci strong divisibility and minimal endpoint arithmetic. It is an exact synthesis, not a priority claim about rank-of-apparition theory.
3. The specific source-locked merger pair `(3,8,3)` and `(1,1,1,2)` is first realized at `F72`. No claim is made that **every** different-content merger is restricted to indices divisible by 72.
4. The infinite first-activation-at-depth-two theorem is explicitly **restricted to alphabet A={1,2,3,5,7,11}**. Only the existence of a new length-two word is assured in the full grammar for that family; new first-order labels outside A are not excluded.
5. The exact maximum length `t+1` applies to histories consisting entirely of label `1` at `F_(6*4^t)`. Mixed-label histories may be longer.
6. The finite census is indices `1..84`; the independent factorizer is certified only on integers less than `2**64`. The infinite-family theorems use modular arithmetic and mathematical proofs, not giant factorization.
7. Lucas and `F_n+1` are descriptive controls, not matched statistical nulls. No exceptionalism, mechanism for quantum phenomena, or broad Substrate universality is inferred.
8. All file work and verification are local; the curated mathematical foundations and earlier v001–v003 releases remain untouched. No GitHub actions.
