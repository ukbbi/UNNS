# Limits, scope and non-claims — FUG-I v005

- **No universal conclusion about the UNNS Substrate.** The exact classification applies to the selected *integer self-substitution* grammar, not automatically to rational non-anchor occurrences or other UNNS operators.
- **Infinite unrestricted delayed-Fibonacci family remains open.** The criterion is exact: for odd r, the first new depth is two iff L_(12r)/2 is squarefree. It is not established that this condition holds for infinitely many odd r.
- **Infinite unrestricted first-depth-one obstruction is proved.** r=7s, s odd supplies a new one-step label 6 and disproves extending the restricted Phase 4 family unchanged to all labels.
- **Finite exact census:** 307 transports through 84 Fibonacci indices. These integers lie within the v004 `<2**64` certified factorization range. Extending to larger indices needs separately verified arithmetic algorithms or modular-only proofs.
- **Source-locked mergers:** the unbounded family is mathematically constructive and depends on known multiplier relations (not newly discovered numerical identities). Fibonacci's ability to absorb every finite modulus via a rank of apparition is classical.
- **Controls are mathematical, not inferential statistics.** The matched integer family 144→288p demonstrates that delayed activation is not Fibonacci-specific in isolation. It is not a statistical null model for physical observations.
- **No claim of new physics.** No experimental dataset is used in Phase 5.
- **No GitHub modification.** Development is local; earlier releases are reference baselines.
