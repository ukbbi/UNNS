2. **Exact matched arithmetic control:** Use a=144 and b=288p for primes p≥5. These have exactly equal square-divisor kernels q(a)=q(b)=12, so identical one-step UNNS label sets. A depth-two history (7,2) is new at every b. This shows the pattern is not exclusive to Fibonacci.

4. **Infinite obstruction:** For odd s, use the Lucas odd-multiplier divisibility theorem to show 7²|L_(84s), while 7 does not divide F_(84s). Thus N=6 appears immediately in the doubled layer.
5. **Avoid unwarranted exceptionalism:** Fibonacci strong divisibility and rank existence already explain some of its structural stability. Do not treat the existence of mergers or delayed activation as uniquely Fibonacci-specific.
6. **Numerical controls do not replace proofs:** finite witness tables are supportive exact examples; infinite claims rely on proof statements in `theory/THEOREMS.md`.
