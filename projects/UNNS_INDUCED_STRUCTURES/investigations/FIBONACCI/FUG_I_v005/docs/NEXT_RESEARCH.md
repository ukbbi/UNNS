# FUG-I after Phase 5 — open, testable next questions

1. **Squarefree Lucas quotient:** Investigate whether infinitely many odd r satisfy `L_(12r)/2` squarefree. This is a number-theoretic question; the current work does not assume a positive answer. Establish exact bounds and partial prime-power obstructions rather than guessing a density.
2. **Higher first activation depth:** Determine whether Fibonacci index-divisibility pairs exhibit first new word depth≥3; distinguish a proof of impossibility from a larger finite census.
3. **Cross-content merger rank spectra:** Compute z(E_pair) for selected independently verified GEO-HD kernel relations and classify their first Fibonacci appearances; avoid calling inherited kernel equalities new.
4. **Rational non-anchor sector:** Develop a separate proposed grammar before transporting integer-history laws to non-anchor UNNS occurrences. Do not infer such extensions from integer self-substitution alone.
5. **Matched sequence controls:** Build valuation-matched, size-matched, and recurrence-aware controls before treating relative branching frequencies as distinctive of Fibonacci.
