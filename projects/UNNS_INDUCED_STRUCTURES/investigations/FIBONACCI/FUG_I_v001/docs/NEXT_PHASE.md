Extend the route search to index ranges `1..72`, `1..96` with exact arithmetic and resource profiling. Avoid claiming absence of paths at unsearched lengths. Record the output indices and all label histories, including routes with non-Fibonacci intermediates.

## P3 — All-index obstructions and constructive families

Study necessary divisibility and valuation conditions for `F_j/F_i=product (N_r+1)^2/N_r` under the sequential admissibility constraints. Look for proof that only finitely many Fibonacci-to-Fibonacci pairs are connected directly, or construct an infinite family. Neither outcome is predetermined.

## P4 — Graph geometry with source/target index labels

Investigate joint structures that retain (a) incoming occurrence fibres, (b) transport under index divisibility, and (c) ambient generative path histories. Determine which commuting diagrams are exact and which involve extra chosen transition conventions. Do not present arbitrary graph decorations as an intrinsic substrate theorem.

## P5 — Enhanced nulls
