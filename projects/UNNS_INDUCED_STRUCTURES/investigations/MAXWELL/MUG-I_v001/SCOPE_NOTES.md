# MUG-I v001 — scope notes (added after review; v001 files unchanged)

1. Whole-lattice versus restricted carries: v001 Sect. 5 states the mapping condition as M in GL_D(Q). For a map of the WHOLE mode lattice into itself the condition is M integral; a rational similarity acts on the finite-index sublattice {m : Mm integral} (e.g. M = I/2 is rational but not whole-lattice). Thm 5.1 classifies rational similarities.
2. Cor 5.2 excludes only whole-lattice irrational carries on the 3-torus. Restricted carries exist for rays and for some rank-2 supports (see ../MUG-I_v002/NOTES.md, Sect. 1–2).
3. Helicity: the label is preserved; integrated helicity is divided by alpha (like the mode action).
4. Prop 3.3 (no history invariant beyond the product) is a statement about final fields from identical initial data; histories remain distinct as label-level structure.
