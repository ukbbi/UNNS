# CIB-I v001 — Research limits and next discriminating test

- **Not a manuscript.** This is an active comparison and theorem register.
- **Not an independent empirical source.** Binary dielectric profiles are conventional analytic model families, chosen as physical controls without tailoring a specific harmonic q to the UNNS diamond, but they are constructed by the investigator rather than sampled from material measurements.
- **No retrospective data presented as a blind test.** The primary media and scores are frozen in `protocol/PROTOCOL.json`. The team made exploratory calculations before final protocol lock; held-out fractions are deterministic mathematical checks, not prospective empirical observations. Post-protocol topology and prime-gate extensions are labeled exploratory.
- **No all-orders photon-selection claim.** A zero first-order material Fourier matrix entry need not imply zero finite-amplitude dynamical mode conversion. Inverse dielectric terms, multiple intermediate Fourier paths and temporal protocols can produce higher-order amplitudes.
- **No physical whole-field T_N transformation.** Only a declared arithmetic relation among integer mode indices is compared with Maxwell's perturbative Fourier graph.
- **No preferential UNNS superiority.** The Fourier q-matched source scramble gives exactly equal first-order aggregate scores. Other arithmetic grammars sometimes exceed UNNS and sometimes underperform, depending on duty fraction. The controls are explanatory, not a degree/path-matched null distribution or p-value test.
- **Basis dependence.** All integer Fourier indices refer to one explicitly fixed 2π physical domain. Changing the physical cell/resolution can change number-theoretic index classification. Do not equate this classification with an intrinsic electromagnetic invariant.
- **Cross-framework caution.** Schrödinger and scalar acoustic first-order Fourier kernel identities are mathematical analogues, not independent experimental confirmation of UNNS.
- **Next priority.** Obtain one measured fixed-geometry dielectric grating (or pre-registered full-vector Maxwell simulation from independent lab parameters), freeze the mode mapping, compare UNNS versus near-grammar, squared scaling, source-shuffled and degree/path-matched nulls on *path-level observables* not determined solely by q, and then validate with unseen data. Reject or retain claims based on the outcome; never tune harmonics to a desired UNNS edge.
