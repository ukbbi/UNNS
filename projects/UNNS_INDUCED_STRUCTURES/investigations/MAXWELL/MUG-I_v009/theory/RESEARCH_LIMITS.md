# MUG-I v009 — Scope, controls, and open questions

**The physical model is not general Maxwell theory.** It is a one-dimensional nondispersive, externally time-modulated, lossless dielectric Maxwell reduction. Its modulation is treated as prescribed; a real material would have dispersion, pump bandwidth, and modulation limits. The working Fourier fields are complex but represent real solutions by taking real parts.

**The coupling is genuinely Maxwell dynamical, but designed.** Grating harmonics (6,28) were chosen to align with one UNNS-mode path; they were not inferred blindly from an independently characterized material. The model drives *mode conversion* rather than acting as the original whole-field T_N pullback. The comparison B uses a different grating (7,27), and the reverse-order control changes the external protocol.

**No unique UNNS mechanism was discovered.** The Maxwell-only Fourier selection rules account for every tested transition, including an infinite family of transitions forbidden by forward UNNS nesting. The UNNS overlay does nevertheless provide an exact way to classify which physically allowed mode transitions also belong to the substrate's own history geometry.

**Perturbative versus exact.** Spatial congruence selection is exact for the time-dependent Maxwell system. The guaranteed nonzero outgoing target follows from a nonvanishing coefficient at second order in η, so it holds for sufficiently small η, not as an unconditional all-amplitude statement. Numerical simulations support the chosen finite η=.009. Fixed-medium eigenstate no-go and co-dilation belong to v008, not to this time-varying scenario.

**No lab validation.** All observations in this release are generated by mathematical derivations and two numerical solvers. There are no physical instrument records or independently observed outcomes.

**Prospective status is qualified.** The 13 inequalities were fixed before the *held-out numerical runs* at (η,T)=(.009,1.7). A preliminary pilot at (.012,2) had already been examined, and the lock was not externally timestamped. Thus this is a reproducible precommitted computational check, not independent experimental validation.

**Natural next hypothesis.** An externally characterized medium (spatial modulation spectrum, polarization coupling, and pump timings fixed without reference to desired UNNS edges) could be used to form a prospective UNNS-index-path forecast. One should compare forecasts against a Maxwell-only Fourier graph, a matched random-index graph, and measured scattering matrices before claiming added predictive advantage.
