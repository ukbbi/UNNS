# Claim boundaries and editorial corrections

## Separate the construction levels

1. **Primitive arithmetic occurrence:** `M ⟂ N = M(N+1)^2/N` on its original domain with rational outcomes allowed.
2. **SELECT-II integer self-substitution:** `T_N(x) = x(N+1)^2/N`, `N | x`, which determines a particular directed history graph.
3. **Sequence-conditioned overlay:** the independently specified external sequence supplies ordering, recurrence or index relations among those histories.
4. **Joint dynamical extension:** recurrence matrices and coordinatewise `T_N` specify an additional state evolution; coordinatewise maps are not uniquely forced by the primitive.
5. **External physics/measurement:** Maxwell equations, material constitutive laws, detector timestamps and experimental protocol remain independent. Arithmetic matches alone are not physical mechanisms.

Other UNNS grammars remain legitimate and may be studied independently; no single historical grammar is a universal definition of the Substrate.

## Required corrections / interpretation controls

- **SIB-I v005, RCS-02:** A constant GCD across recurrence evolution is not a theorem for every linear recurrence. State-coordinate GCD is preserved by **integer unimodular** maps (with integer inverse); the Fibonacci/Pell/Tribonacci scope and positivity/admissibility hypotheses must be retained. For example, `a_{n+2}=a_{n+1}+2a_n` with start `(a_0,a_1)=(1,2)` yields `(a_1,a_2)=(2,4)`, increasing GCD from 1 to 2.
- **SIB-I v003:** Equality of E3 and Efull within 1..65,536 is **finite exact**, not a universal theorem.
- **SIB-I v001/v002:** Repeated totient values affect observation-weighted counts; missing same-exponent/same-size controls for powers of two must not be interpreted as negative evidence.
- **SIB-I v004:** Adjacent Fibonacci/Lucas word exclusion follows from coprimality; it is not specific to the arithmetic grammar. Alternative divisibility grammars supply controls.
- **SIB-I v005:** `R(T_Ns)=T_N(Rs)` for linear homogeneous R holds where both sides are defined. It does not imply nontrivial curvature, a physical interaction, or the uniqueness of a joint grammar. The verified factorization counts distinct ancestor *states*, not all interleavings of ordered histories.
- **MUG-I v007/v008:** The global `N | q` Bloch veto in an earlier numerical construction is an added filtering rule, not a consequence of exact Maxwell dynamics or the original UNNS integer grammar. The v008 fixed-medium obstruction should be cited with its medium and on-shell eigenmode hypotheses.
- **MUG-I v009/CIB-I:** Constructed traveling dielectric pumps yield Maxwell couplings and also non-UNNS routes. Cross-grammar/q-preserving nulls block unique UNNS mechanism claims.
- **QTI-I v001–v003:** Time-tag grid, clock reconstruction and detector-channel protocol explain many apparent ancestry or coincidence statistics. The `ps` channel must not be called an authenticated herald or fidelity measurement. v003's cached internal checks do not replace replay from original `.pt` files.
- **Intrinsic vs selected / joint:** Full endpoint equivalence does not imply path equivalence, unique future selection, common refinement, or physical information transfer.
- **External physical data:** Analysis depends on the data's representation and frozen instrument definitions. Statistical association does not prove the proposed substrate mechanism.

## Status language

- `PROVED`: a mathematical derivation under explicit assumptions.
- `VERIFIED-EXACT`/`FINITE-EXACT`: exhaustive or independently checked finite computation; not universal without a separate theorem.
- `NUMERICAL`/`COMPUTED`: software experiment or physical-data calculation.
- `EXPLORATORY`: hypothesis or descriptive observation.
- `OPEN`/`NOT ESTABLISHED`: construction, selection, causal or physical interpretation remains unresolved.

The status of an older result must not be silently upgraded by a later interpretive synthesis.
