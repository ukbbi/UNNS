## v007: ingredients and unverified connections

Technical ingredients of the new proofs are elementary and self-contained: plane lattice geometry (column structure of a coset of a rank-2 lattice in a box, point-reflection symmetry of rectangles between two lattice points, the integer height/basis argument in the hollow box), exact rational polygon clipping, binary codes, circuits and cosets, linear algebra over `Q`.

**Unverified directions mentioned, not cited as support (no reference asserted, no search made in v007):** (i) the relation of the strip construction and of the Graver-cell structure to lattice triangulations, "fundamental parallelepipeds" and Hilbert bases of 2-dimensional cones; (ii) the relation of the circuit/coset description of binary cells to cube complexes, binary matroids and cycle spaces (circuits of a binary matroid are the minimal supports of the cycle space — standard matroid language — and cosets of its subspaces are standard linear algebra); (iii) the possible overlap of the B-COSET / B-DELTA statements with known facts about zonotopal cell structures of lattice-ideal fibres. Any of these may already exist in the toric-geometry / Markov-basis / matroid literature.
