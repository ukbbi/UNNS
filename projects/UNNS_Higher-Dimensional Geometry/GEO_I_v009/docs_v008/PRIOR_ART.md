# GEO-I v008 — Prior art and what is (not) claimed

Status vocabulary: **record seen** (a bibliographic record or abstract appeared in a search in this session; the work itself was **not opened or read**), **carried** (listed in earlier GEO-I releases, not re-fetched), **unverified** (general knowledge; check before relying). Nothing is invented; **no priority claim is made**; the review below is **equivalence-based** (by mathematical content), and it is incomplete.

## Not claimed
* No claim that any of G8-T1, G8-S, G8-R1, B-LOW, B-COMMON, B-SERIES, the nullity-3/4 classification or the RM family is absent from the literature. B-LOW (rank ≥ 3 of every collision) in particular is a short argument and may well be known in other language.
* G8-C1, G8-T2, G8-C2, G8-C3, G8-T3 are **open** in this release.
* Nothing here concerns a physical interpretation of UNNS or any bridge to spacetime or FIELD-I predictions.
