# GEO-I v010 — Prior art and what is (not) claimed

Vocabulary: **abstract opened** (the bibliographic/abstract page was fetched and read; full text **not** read), **carried** (listed in earlier releases, not re-fetched), **unverified** (general knowledge). No reference is invented; **no priority or novelty claim is made.** The review is by mathematical content and incomplete.

## Sources actually examined in this release (abstract level only)
* B. Frederickson, L. Michel, *Circuit decompositions of binary matroids* (arXiv:2306.14236). Abstract page opened. It studies the minimum number of disjoint circuits needed to decompose an Eulerian binary matroid and bounds it; it states **no** uniqueness or non-uniqueness criterion for circuit decompositions. Relation to G10-E1: related theme (decompositions into disjoint circuits), different question.
* E. G. Mphako, *Circuit and cocircuit partitions of binary matroids*, Czechoslovak Math. J. 56 (2006) — abstract page (EuDML) opened: constructs a class of binary matroids with a cocircuit partition. Full text not read; no statement about two partitions of one set.

Graver basis (J. E. Graver, Math.
