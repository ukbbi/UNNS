## v006 additions
No new external dependency is used in any proof. Technical ingredients are elementary: plane lattice geometry, Pareto/“sail” structure of a coset quadrant, exact rational polygon clipping, linear algebra over `Q`, and binary codes/circuits (basic matroid terminology).

**Unverified directions mentioned, not cited as support (no reference asserted):** the relation of the corner-sail construction to Klein polygons / Hirzebruch–Jung continued fractions and to Hilbert bases of two-dimensional cones; the relation of the 0/1 binary-box theory to cube complexes, binary matroids and circuit/cocircuit structure of cycle spaces. These may exist in the literature on toric geometry, lattice polytopes, Markov bases and matroid theory; **no search was made in v006**.

## Not found / not claimed
* **No priority claim.** The literature check remains incomplete.
