# D4-T2 / D4-T6 — source thresholds

* gcd reduction (v003): for a fibre only gcd(m, P*) matters, P* = lcm_c P(c), P(c)=prod N^{c_N}.  Exhaustive classification of a fibre is therefore over the divisors of P*.
* Saturation bound: if P(c) | m for every content c of the fibre then every arrangement of every content is admissible (v001) — see GLOBAL_CONNECTIVITY T6a.  For the family 2^4 3^j <-> 1·8^2 3^j the saturation bound has exponents (6, j) in (2,3) (results/DICHOTOMY.json), and the true connectivity boundary is far below it: 3 | m or 16 | m.  So the product/lcm bound is not sharp; the sharp boundary is an exposure condition (T7), not an all-arrangements condition.
