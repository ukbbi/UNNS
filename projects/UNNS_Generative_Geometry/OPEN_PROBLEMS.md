# Open questions — GEO-HD-1B and beyond

1. **Nontrivial 3D Graver-cell coverage.** For `x_3=(2·5·11)^4/3`, classify `H`, candidate `U`, cell incidence and uncovered regions with an exactly stated cell grammar. Check its consistency with Paper A's 2D definition.
2. **4D extension.** For `x_4=(2·5·11·17)^4/3`, decide whether the 3D phenomenon persists or changes. Do not infer topology from a 3D projection.
3. **Face-to-face embedding.** Are intersections unions of shared faces in the chosen realization? Separate abstract complex topology and embedded geometry.
4. **Higher homology.** Compute integral `H_1`, `H_2` and, where appropriate, `H_3`, with reproducible boundary matrices; do not report a cavity from a hull deficiency alone.
5. **Cross-fibre cellular transport.** Which SELECT-II transitions yield well-defined, geometry-preserving maps between `E(m)` and `E(t)`? Does index inclusion imply cell preservation under a shared grammar? Find counterexamples.
6. **Arithmetic recombination.** Find exact number-theoretic obstructions or equivalence criteria for distinct SELECT-II histories to merge in a target.
7. **Projection faithfulness.** For a finite labelled graph find minimal coordinate subsets that distinguish vertices and edges, and explicit collisions under smaller projections.
8. **FIELD-I strands.** Under the exact eventual scaling hypotheses, classify strand intersections and determine what is new beyond translating the known scaling theorem.
9. **Literature comparison.** Contrast with D_k/C_k roots, toric and Graver geometry, zonotopal/parallelotope complexes, directed-path unfolding, and coding theory with exact matching hypotheses.

Do not promote exploratory computations to universal theorems.
