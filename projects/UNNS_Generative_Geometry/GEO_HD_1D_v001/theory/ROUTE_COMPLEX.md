# Route complex (HD-1D v001)
**Objects.** (1) *State cubes*: cells (m; {N_1..N_k}) with N_1...N_k | m (D-T1(c)) - the commutation geometry at a state; this is a higher-dimensional-automaton-like structure whose independence relation is state dependent (A,B independent at m iff AB | m).
(2) *Swap complex* S(m_0,t): vertices = admissible histories from m_0 to t; edges = legal swaps; cubes = sets of legal non-overlapping swaps (Independence Lemma); permutation cells = windows with product | state.
**What is proved.** Disjoint-swap cubes are completely determined by pairwise-local data (swap legality at the state before the block) - there is no obstruction to completing squares or higher cubes from non-overlapping swaps. Overlapping swaps can fail (hexagon, D-T3(c)).
**What differs from the Boolean picture.** The orthant constraint removes vertices of the Boolean cube of a content: P_u = {S : u + sum_{i in S} Delta_i >= 0}. Swap classes are the connected components of the monotone-path space of this induced cube complex under legal square flips (for contents of distinct labels); a hole (resource contention) can disconnect them (D-T5).
**Interface to HD-1E (not developed).** A history is a walk of valuation vectors; a family of histories running side by side is a family of strands in the same orthant; FIELD-I trajectories live in valuation space as well. Any identification of SELECT-II routes with FIELD-I strands needs an explicit bridge definition - none is assumed here.
**Link to E2 (not used).** The relation lattices / Graver cells of E2 are the *state-wise* geometry; the swap complex is a different, route-level geometry.
