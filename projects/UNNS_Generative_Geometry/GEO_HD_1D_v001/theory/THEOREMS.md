## D-T0  Predecessors and the history-count recurrence (PROVED)
(a) m --N--> t  iff  d := N+1 satisfies d^2 | t and m = t (d-1)/d^2.  (b) Hence every state t has exactly #{d >= 2 : d^2 | t} incoming edges, one per d; t is squarefree iff it has none. (c) H(t) = 1 for squarefree t and H(t) = sum_{d>=2, d^2|t} H(t(d-1)/d^2) otherwise. (d) T_N is injective in m, T_N(m) >= 4m, so every history to t has length <= log_4(t/m_0) and H(t) is finite.
*Proof.* (a) If m = N k then t = k (N+1)^2, so (N+1)^2 | t and m = N t/(N+1)^2. Conversely, for d^2 | t put N = d-1, m = N t/d^2; then N | m (m = N * (t/d^2)) and T_N(m) = t. The correspondence d <-> (N,m) is bijective. (c) A complete history of non-squarefree t has positive length; removing its last edge gives a complete history of the predecessor, and the map (history) -> (last edge, history of the predecessor) is a bijection. For squarefree t only the empty history exists. (d) (N+1)^2/N = N+2+1/N >= 4 with equality only for N = 1, strictly increasing in N, so N is determined by the ratio. QED
*Corroboration.* V1: the recurrence equals an independent forward dynamic programme for all t <= 100,000 (fast) / 373,248 (full); explicit enumeration agrees.

## D-W  Valuation-walk model (PROVED; inherited arithmetic, one line)
Put u = u(m) in Z_{>=0}^{primes}. A label N is admissible at u iff u_p >= v_p(N) for all p, equivalently u + Delta(N) >= 0 (primes with Delta_p >= 0 are automatically fine). A history is a walk in the nonnegative orthant with step set {Delta(N)}, and the endpoint is the end of the walk. Total displacement telescopes (as the specification notes), so the walk's *route* - not its endpoint - carries the history.
*Consequences.* (i) a swap of adjacent A, B at u is legal iff the parallelogram u, u+Delta(A), u+Delta(B), u+Delta(A)+Delta(B) lies in the orthant; (ii) the set of histories with a given source and content is the set of orderings of a multiset of vectors whose partial sums stay in the orthant; (iii) this is exactly the structure of execution paths of concurrent processes with counting semaphores (prime p = a resource of capacity u_p; a label divisible by p consumes it; a label N with p | N+1 produces it). Neighbouring literature (not used, not read in detail): directed algebraic topology of concurrency (Fajstrup-Goubault-Haucourt-Mimram-Sagnier, *Directed Algebraic Topology and Concurrency*, Springer 2016, title seen only), higher-dimensional automata (e.g. arXiv:1506.02270, title seen only), Mazurkiewicz traces.

## D-T1  Arithmetic diamond criterion (PROVED)
For A, B >= 1 and a state m:
(a) *Ordered route.* m --A--> --B--> exists iff  m_{A->B} | m, where m_{A->B} = A B / gcd(B, (A+1)^2). So the admitting states are exactly the multiples of m_{A->B}.
(b) *Diamond.* Both routes A,B and B,A exist iff A B | m. Equivalently the specification's p-adic conditions u_p >= a_p, u_p >= b_p, u_p + 2v_p(A+1) - a_p >= b_p, u_p + 2v_p(B+1) - b_p >= a_p hold for all p.
(c) *Higher-order cells.* For labels N_1..N_k (a multiset), all arrangements are admissible from m iff N_1 ... N_k | m. In that case the 2^k states reached by executing a subset S of the labels (in any order) are integral, and the Boolean cube of subsets is a "state cube" of the system.
(d) *Swap legality.* If a history passes through state m before positions i, i+1 with labels A != B (so A,B is admissible), the swap is legal iff A B | m.
*Proof.* (a) A | m: write m = A k, T_A(m) = k (A+1)^2. B | k(A+1)^2 iff B/g | k with g = gcd(B,(A+1)^2) (cancel g; B/g and (A+1)^2/g are coprime). So m = A k with (B/g) | k, i.e. (AB/g) | m. (b) lcm(AB/g_1, AB/g_2) = AB / gcd(g_1,g_2) with g_1 = gcd(B,(A+1)^2) | B, g_2 = gcd(A,(B+1)^2) | A, and gcd(g_1,g_2) | gcd((A+1)^2, A) = 1, so the lcm is AB. Since "both routes" = (m_{A->B} | m and m_{B->A} | m) = (lcm | m), (b) follows. The p-adic form: for p | A, p | B: conditions give u >= a + b (v_p(A+1) = v_p(B+1) = 0); for p dividing exactly one of A, B, say p | A: u >= a (the other inequalities are weaker); for p dividing neither: vacuous; this is v_p(AB) in every case. (c) Sufficiency: if Pi N_i | m then for any order and j, u_p + sum_{i<j} Delta_p(N_i) >= sum_{i>=j} v_p(N_i) >= v_p(N_j), since terms Delta_p >= -v_p(N_i). Necessity: fix p and order first the labels divisible by p; the last of them needs u_p - (sum of earlier v_p) >= v_p, i.e. u_p >= sum over labels of v_p(N_i) = v_p(Pi N_i) (labels not divisible by p have v_p = 0). The cube statement: for ΠN | m every subset product divides m and m_S = m Pi_{i in S}(N_i+1)^2/N_i is an integer. (d) is (b) with the observation that the original order is admissible. QED
*Corroboration.* V2: (a) and (b) on 405,000 triples (m <= 200, A,B <= 45; m <= 500 in --full), the p-adic form equals (b), lcm identity for A,B < 150, (c) on 2,406 multiset cases.

## D-T2  Multiplicity (PROVED lower bound; FINITE-EXACT values)
(a) H(72^r) >= C(2r,r) for r >= 1. (b) H(72) = 4, H(72^2) = 100, H(72^3) = 3601, H(72^4) = 194,864, H(72^5) = 14,177,856, H(72^6) = 1,258,011,667 (recurrence; r <= 3 also by independent forward DP). The bound C(2r,r) = 2, 6, 20, 70, 252, 924 is far from sharp. From source 1 alone: 2, 16, 425, 18,377, 1,314,291, 114,913,251.
*Proof of (a).* From 1 apply label 1 r times (reaching 4^r = 2^{2r}). Any arrangement of r labels 1 and r labels 2 is admissible: before the (b+1)-st label 2 the 2-adic valuation is 2r + 2a - b >= 2r - b >= r + 1 >= 1. The endpoint is 4^r * 4^r * (9/2)^r = 72^r. Distinct arrangements are distinct label sequences from one source. QED

## D-T3  Route cubes (PROVED)
**Independence Lemma.** Let h be a history and i_1 < ... < i_d positions with i_{k+1} >= i_k + 2, such that each pair (N_{i_k}, N_{i_k+1}) consists of distinct labels whose swap is legal in h. Then for every choice b in {0,1}^d the history h_b obtained by performing the chosen swaps is admissible, has the same endpoint, and the h_b are pairwise distinct. They form a d-cube whose edges are single legal swaps.
*Proof.* Induct on blocks. Both orders of block k end at the same state, so the state before block k+1 does not depend on the choices made in earlier blocks; hence the legality of block k+1's swap (a statement about that state only, D-T1(d)) holds in every variant, and all other labels are unchanged. Distinctness: the label sequences differ. QED
**D-T3.** For every r >= 1 the complete histories of 72^r contain an r-cube: prefix 1^r, then r blocks, each independently 12 or 21. The block j starts at 4^r 18^{j-1}, whose 2-adic valuation is >= 2r >= 1, so 2 | m and AB = 2 | m, hence the swap is legal; each block multiplies by 18, so all 2^r histories end at 4^r 18^r = 72^r. By the Independence Lemma these are an r-cube.
(b) *The cube dimension of 72^r is not r.* Maximum cube dimension over *all* complete histories of 72^r is 1, 2, 4, 5 for r = 1, 2, 3, 4 (FINITE-EXACT; r = 4 stored, not independently recomputed). Explicit 4-cube in 72^3: source 3, labels (3,1,2,1,2,1,2,1) with disjoint legal swaps at positions 0, 2, 4, 6. Trivial upper bound: cube dimension <= floor(l/2) with l the history length.
(c) *Local does not imply global at overlaps.* The Independence Lemma covers non-overlapping swaps. For overlapping swaps the hexagon of three distinct labels needs all six arrangements, which by D-T1(c) happens iff N_1 N_2 N_3 | m: history (2; 1,2,4) has both adjacent swaps legal but only 3 of 6 arrangements admissible; at m = 60 the labels 2, 6, 10 are pairwise swappable but 120 does not divide 60. (Counts: 319 triples with pairwise diamonds but no triple cell for m < 400.)
*Corroboration.* V3 (cubes r <= 6 fast, 8 full; symbolic block condition r <= 60), V4 (72^3 4-cube), V7 (Independence Lemma on all legal non-overlapping swap pairs of the histories of 72^2 and 72^3; overlapping failures).

## D-T4  Information carried by histories (PROVED)
**Lemma V (fibre = half-valuation).** D(m) = {d >= 2 : v_p(d) <= e_p(m) for all p}; so D(m) = D(m') iff e(m) = e(m') iff s(m) = s(m'). The fibre forgets the parity of every valuation.
**Lemma L (labels are visible).** If A != B are both admissible at m then D(T_A m) != D(T_B m). More precisely there is a prime p with | u_p(T_A m) - u_p(T_B m) | >= 2, hence e_p differs and the index p^k (k = the larger e_p) lies in exactly one of the two fibres.
*Proof.* A+1 != B+1, so some prime p has v_p(A+1) != v_p(B+1); say v_p(A+1) > v_p(B+1) >= 0. Then p does not divide A and Delta_p(A) = 2 v_p(A+1) >= 2. If v_p(B+1) >= 1 then Delta_p(B) = 2 v_p(B+1) and the difference is >= 2; if v_p(B+1) = 0 then Delta_p(B) = -v_p(B) <= 0 and the difference is >= 2 v_p(A+1) >= 2. A difference >= 2 in u_p gives different floors. QED
**Theorem (faithfulness).** For a fixed source, the fibre sequence F(h) determines h. *Proof.* Induct on the length: the state m_{j-1} is known; by Lemma L the fibre D(m_j) determines N_j among the labels admissible at m_{j-1}. QED. (Corroboration V6: 7,666 histories, 0 collisions.)
**Corollary (what the endpoint forgets).** Histories with the same source and endpoint have pairwise different fibre sequences (even when they are swap-equivalent); the endpoint keeps only the last entry. In the benchmark of 72: rho_2(h1) = (0,1,1,1), rho_2(h2) = (0,1,0,1).
**Locality.** A legal swap at position i changes exactly one entry of the fibre sequence (the intermediate one, by Lemma L: nonempty change) - V6 verifies on 192 swaps. Hence what swaps preserve is: source, content, endpoint, and all fibres except one; the occurrence signature rho is never a swap invariant. The diamond m -> 4m -> 18m, m -> 9m/2 -> 18m (every even m) is the case A,B = 1,2.
**Event locality.** If d is lost at a step with label N then gcd(d,N) > 1; if d is born then gcd(d,N+1) > 1 (some prime q | d has e_q change, forcing Delta_q != 0). Label 1 never causes a loss and raises e_2 by exactly 1. (V6: 3,920 transitions.)

## D-T5  Route equivalence is strictly finer than (source, content) (PROVED family; minimal cases FINITE-EXACT)
Let m_0 = 14 q with q squarefree and coprime to 210. The label multiset {2,3,14} has exactly two admissible arrangements, (14,3,2) and (2,3,14), both ending at 5400 q, and neither has a legal swap. So they have the same source, content and endpoint but lie in different swap classes.
*Proof (q = 1).* From 14 the labels dividing 14 among {2,3,14} are 2 and 14. After 14: 225, label 2 does not divide 225, label 3 does: (14,3,2): 14, 225, 1200, 5400. After 2: 63; 14 does not divide 63, 3 does: (2,3,14): 14, 63, 336, 5400. Swaps: (14,3) needs 3 | 14, no; (3,2) needs 6 | 225, no; (2,3): 3 | 14, no; (3,14): 42 | 63, no. For general q the prime factors of q play no role: admissibility depends on valuations at 2, 3, 7 only, and AB | state iff AB | state/q. QED
Mechanism (D-W): the prime 2 has capacity 1 at the source; both 2 and 14 consume it; only label 3 produces it (N+1 = 4). Exactly one of {2,14} can run before 3 - a resource contention. Other minimal cases: source 2, content {1,2,3,8}, endpoint 1944; source 15, content {2,4,5,5,15}, endpoint 72^3 (two classes of 4 histories). In the census (non-squarefree t in [4,6000], H(t) <= 4000, 2,354 endpoints) the split endpoints are exactly 1944, 2400, 4050, 4320, 5400 (FINITE-EXACT). Within 72^r: none for r = 1, 2; one group for r = 3.
