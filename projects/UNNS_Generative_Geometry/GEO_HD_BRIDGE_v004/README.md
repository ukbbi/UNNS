# GEO_HD_BRIDGE_v004 — Activation of dormant arithmetic relations

Decisive target: **A4-T3**, the dynamical activation criterion. Question: when does an *admissible transformation* (the actual FIELD-I map `C`; the frozen SELECT-II integer rule is not extended) turn a dormant primitive relation into a realised one, and does that change the higher-dimensional topology?

## Result in brief
* **Proved (A4-T1/T2).** For `x=P²/ℓ`, `y=(Pq)²/ℓ`: `D(x)={P}`, `D(y)={P,p_iq}`; the relations `e_i−e_j` are dormant at `x`, active at `y` (activation, not creation); `K_move(y)=K_{k+1}` without 2-cells, `β₁=k(k−1)/2`. Machine-confirmed (k=2..5, B4-1 `55²/3→935²/3`, 60+ further members).
* **Proved (A4-T3 structure).** `D` is monotone along every C-orbit, so realised relations persist and every activation is a D-event; the orbit from `x` is an explicit arithmetic progression until its first event; exact necessary conditions and a blocking criterion for reaching `y`.
* **Finite-exact (A4-T3 existence).** `y` **is** reached by actual C-orbits in 40 of 993 tested members (e.g. `3025/3 → 12100/3` in 8 steps, `β₁ 0→1`; `k=3, ℓ=7`: 375 steps, `β₁ 0→3`); 929 are provably blocked; 24 unresolved; no overshoot. Step-level activation without denominator excursion occurs in the ℓ=5 tower (`β₁ 1→4`) and at ℓ=29.
* **Counterexamples.** "q²>ℓ ⇒ unreachable" (my first conjecture) is false; "activation ⇒ Betti change" is false (80 of 474 scan events leave Betti numbers unchanged); primitivity and `K_move` topology are not persistent.
* **Towers (A4-T5/T6, finite-exact, no extrapolation).** G=11: `β₁=8, β₂=17`, `β₃` period-5 growth to k=45; G=5: `(1,6,5,0)` for 7≤k≤45; ℓ=29 is G=29, `(P,ρ²)=(35,900)`.
* **History (A4-T7).** `K_move` of integer endpoints is contractible while their history complexes have 2–3 components: proved obstruction; nothing identified.
* **Open.** Infinitely many reached members; no-overshoot; closed form of the first event; proofs of the tower Betti laws.
