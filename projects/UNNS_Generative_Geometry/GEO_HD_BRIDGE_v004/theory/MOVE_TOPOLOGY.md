# Topology of K_move across states

* **Family (PROVED).** `K_move(x)=pt`, `K_move(y)=K_{k+1}`, `β₁(y)=k(k−1)/2`, `β_{≥2}=0` (INFINITE_FAMILIES). Counting: `β₁ = C(k+1,2) − k` (edges minus a spanning tree).
* **Integer states are topologically trivial (PROVED).** For integer `m` (`b=1`): `Λ_1=Z^k`, `F∪{0}` = whole box, hull rule = grid adjacency, and `K_move(m)` = union of grid cubes avoiding the corner `0`. Cover it by the boxes `{x_i≥1}`; all non-empty intersections are boxes, nerve = simplex ⇒ contractible (nerve lemma). Verified on 9 integers (Z7).
* **Topology is not monotone along C-orbits.** `D` and the realised relation set grow, but `K_move` can lose cycles: `3025/3 → 12100/3` has `β₁=1`; the same orbit is integral at `12100` with Betti `(1,0,0)`. In the orbit scan (`data/ACTIVATION_SCAN.json`), `57` of `474` step-activation events *lower* `β₁`.
* **Activation does not necessarily change the Betti vector (COUNTEREXAMPLE, FINITE-EXACT).** Of 474 step-level activation events on 382 orbits (ℓ=5,7,11,13; `S<1500` in the modular class; 24 steps), `394` change the Betti vector and `80` do not. First unchanged example: ℓ=5, `S=36`, step 3→4: `(1,0)→(1,0,0)` while cells go `[2,1]→[8,10,3]` (a filled complex stays contractible). So "activation ⇒ topology change" is **false in general**; it is true for the A4 family (by A4-T2) and for the ℓ=5 tower step 5→6 (`β₁ 1→4`).
* **Creation without activation.** In the ℓ=11 tower no dormant relation exists (`dormant=0` for all `k≤45`); every Betti change there is creation-realisation. The two mechanisms are distinct.
