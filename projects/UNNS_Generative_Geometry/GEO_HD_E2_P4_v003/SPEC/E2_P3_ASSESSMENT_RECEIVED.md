A further result worth developing from this witness
The new specimen supports an infinite family of FIELD-I containment failures, not merely a finite collection of numerical examples.

\[

\[

\[
\boxed{

Proof outline

\[
G(x_{a,b,c})=\gcd(\ldots,27,90,\ldots)=9.

are eligible and together attain the maximum allowed exponent of every active prime. Therefore
\[
L(x_{a,b,c})=R.
