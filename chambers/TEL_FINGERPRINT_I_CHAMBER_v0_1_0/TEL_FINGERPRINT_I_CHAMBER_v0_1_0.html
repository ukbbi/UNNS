METHOD LOCK

Each branch is represented by ten physically bounded defect coordinates: probability imbalance, terminal closure defect, process infidelity, Choi mixing, Choi entropy, Choi-entanglement loss, three PTM contraction defects, and nonunital displacement. Pair distances are equal-coordinate RMS distances. Route labels remain categorical and never enter the geometry.

λ-level states

CLOSEDAll six pair distances ≤ closed threshold.
DEFORMEDNot closed, but the four-route graph remains connected.
BIFURCATEDTwo or three connected components.
FRAGMENTEDFour singleton components.

Mandatory internal controls

Route-label permutation invariance · deterministic λ thinning · leave-one-coordinate-out sensitivity · leave-one-realization-out retrieval when identifiable.

Scientific boundary

The chamber evaluates structural equivalence under the declared fingerprint. It does not reconstruct quantum states, certify CPTP maps, calculate fidelity from counts, or prove a fundamental transition.
