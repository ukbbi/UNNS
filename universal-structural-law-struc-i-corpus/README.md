## 🧪 Structural Ladder Corpus

The `structural_ladder_corpus/` directory contains ordered datasets ("ladders") derived from multiple domains, including:

- Atomic and nuclear energy levels  
- Molecular and condensed matter systems  
- Random matrix eigenvalue spectra  
- Cosmological signals (CMB)  
- Planetary and geophysical structures  
- Adversarial synthetic configurations  

Each ladder represents an ordered sequence of values subjected to structural analysis.
